**In plain terms:** a new presentation, or one redesigned whole, starts as a *sketch*. The designer starts a session of its own, the *sketch session*, and hands it only the asker's words, the takeaways and where the piece is shown. With no method, no design knowledge, no library and no brand, it draws every page outside the engine. The designer rebuilds the sketch at once as the piece, by its own method and design knowledge, with the brand the piece names and the project's libraries, and writes the reasons. Where they cannot carry a part, the rebuild draws the nearest that keeps the part's meaning, never a bend. What was lacking goes to its owner, as a request to the owner of a library or a brand, or as a need for a tool the engine lacks, and the brief's reason for that part names the lack and its owner. No review reads the sketch. The one round of [visual-communication:DEC-070-ship-call-and-one-round] runs on the rebuilt piece. At the ship call the person sees the sketch beside the piece, and may ask for mends toward it. The sketch stays in the agent workspace and is never committed. Other kinds of work keep the designer's method unless the person asks for a sketch, and the person may skip the sketch on any piece. The evidence is two presentations and one person.

## Context

[visual-communication:DEC-030-design-agents] has the designer design a piece by a method. It names the content's structure before it chooses a form, chooses the form by its job, and takes type, colour and space from the brand (point 3). [visual-communication:DEC-070-ship-call-and-one-round] has the person make the ship call on a piece, after one round of mends. It left a free draft first to a record of its own, once a test said one should (DEC-070, Implications, "Left open").

The tests behind DEC-070 had shown free drafts reaching the person's line. A fresh session, given only a presentation's words and its brief and no design guidance, drew three designs outside the engine. A second fresh session drew another presentation three ways. The person shipped all six, and nothing else in those tests. The capability's own drawing did not ship. Nor did any round of the tests' loop under a critic, its first among them, drawn before any critic read it (DEC-070, Context).

The test of [project-kit#3470](https://github.com/aleskalfas/project-kit/issues/3470) then ran the capability's own agents on the same two presentations. Each of its questions was read against a pass fixed before it ran. Its results are in the scratchpad note `design-review-ship-call` of the repository the capability is authored in ([project-kit#3468](https://github.com/aleskalfas/project-kit/issues/3468)):
- **Drawing freely in the engine did not reach the person's line.** The capability's designer drew the first presentation freely, in the engine, by its method and with one brand. The person shipped neither that drawing nor the version its round mended. Each was better than the other in some parts and worse in others.
- **A shipped free draft survived a rebuild once in two.** The designer rebuilt one free draft of each presentation that the person had shipped, as a piece, with that brand and the libraries.
  - The first rebuild shipped, as its draft had.
  - The second fell short. A sequence whose colours carried its meaning lost that meaning, and running text was drawn at the screen's size for a piece shown in a room. After one round on the person's own notes, the person called it shippable save one page, whose text needed a size the brand gave a piece's own components no way to reach.
- **What the rebuilds could not carry lay partly in the designer.** Dropping the meaning the colours carried is a bend DEC-030 point 4 forbids: the designer carries such a difference another way, or stops. Drawing running text at the screen's size for a room is a choice of the type's role for where the piece is shown, which is the designer's.
- **The rest lay in the brand, the engine and the libraries.** The brand lacked sizes and styles of type that a piece's own components could reach, and colours. The engine lacked tools of drawing. A library drew only the page's layout and a few blocks. The rest was drawn as local components, and some of these were general enough for other pieces.

**How far it goes.** Two pieces, both presentations that explain, drawn with one brand and judged by one person.
- **The condition tested.** What reached the person's line was drawn by a fresh session with no design guidance, outside the engine, from the words and the brief. The first round of the tests' loop under a critic was drawn outside the engine too, and did not ship, so drawing outside the engine is not alone what made the drafts work. Nothing shows which part of the condition did: the fresh session, the lack of guidance, the lack of a library and a brand, or drawing before the content's structure was mapped.
- **A guided first drawing.** Whether the designer, by its method and with its design knowledge, would draw a first drawing as good is untested. The one guided drawing in the evidence, the designer's in the engine, did not reach the person's line.
- **Which drafts were rebuilt.** Only drafts the person shipped were rebuilt, and one rebuild of two shipped. No rebuild of a draft the person would not ship was tried.
- **The round.** The round that brought the second rebuild near the person's line mended the person's notes, not the review's findings. Nothing tested a review of a rebuild against its draft.
- **Other kinds, and other brands.** No piece of another kind of work, such as a figure or a poster, was drawn. The one brand lacked much the rebuilds asked of it.

The words it uses:
- **The person, the asker, the ship call and the round** are DEC-070's. The person is whom the designer works for, and the asker whoever asked for the piece. The ship call is the decision that a piece goes to its audience as it stands. A round is one mend by the designer, then a review of every page.
- **The engine** is the capability's code that computes a piece's scene from its content and its libraries ([visual-communication:DEC-001-visual-communication-discipline] point 2).
- **A library** is a named, versioned set that pieces share, and a brand is one. A **local component** is one made inside a piece, for that piece alone (DEC-001 points 4 and 5).
- **A presentation** is a piece shown to an audience a page at a time, such as a deck at a talk. It is the kind of work the tests drew. Whether a piece is one, the designer reads from its top component and from where it is shown (DEC-001 points 3 and 4).
- **The agent workspace** is the folder at a checkout's root where agents keep what they write only to get the work done. Nothing in it is committed, every agent may write there, and a capability keeps what must last under a folder named after itself (the permissions README, "The agent workspace").
- **A sketch** is a drawing of a whole piece, made outside the engine by the sketch session from the piece's words, its takeaways and where it is shown alone (point 1). The tests called it a free draft. "Draft" already names a piece's state ([visual-communication:DEC-003-change-over-time] point 8) and the words the designer drafts (DEC-070 point 4), so this record calls it a sketch.
- **The sketch session** is an agent session the designer starts for the sketch alone, from an agent definition of the capability's own, which holds nothing of the work before it (point 1).
- **The asker's text** is the words the asker gives for a piece before it is drawn (point 2).
- **The rebuild** is the designer's drawing of a sketch as the piece itself (point 4).
- **A stand-in** is the nearest that keeps a part's meaning, which the rebuild draws where nothing the designer may use draws the part as the sketch does (point 5).
- **A request to its owner** and **a need** keep the meanings they have. A request goes to the owner of a library the designer may not write, a brand among them (DEC-030 point 4). A need names a tool the engine lacks, and goes to the person as a question that names what the design needs (DEC-070 point 5).

## Decision

**A new presentation, or one redesigned whole, starts as a sketch that a session of its own draws unguided outside the engine, and the designer rebuilds it at once as the piece, by its method and with the piece's brand and the project's libraries, and sets the sketch beside the piece at the person's ship call.**

### 1. A new presentation starts with a sketch, which the sketch session draws

- **What it is.** A drawing of every page of the piece, in the shape its pages take where it is shown. The session draws it in a medium it draws freely and the person can open, such as a web page it writes by hand. The engine computes none of it, and none of the capability's formats, commands or checks apply to it.
- **The sketch session draws it.** The designer starts the sketch session for it. It hands the session only the piece's words and its takeaways (point 2), and where the piece is shown. It hands it no method, no design knowledge, no library and no brand, and the session works outside the engine. Where the designer cannot start a session, it asks whoever dispatched it to start one, and hands them the same, as it does for the review (DEC-030 point 2).
- **A definition of its own.** The sketch session runs from an agent definition of the capability's own. Its tools only read the files the designer hands it and write its sketch into the sketch's folder (point 3). Its body is near empty, and holds no method and no design knowledge.
- **Isolation, and where it rests on restraint.** Where a harness cannot narrow what the session reads, its isolation rests on its restraint, as the review's reading order does (DEC-070 point 5). Its handoff says what it was given and anything else it read, so a breach shows.
- **No review and no round.** No review reads the sketch, and no round mends it.
- **Which pieces.** A new presentation, and a presentation redesigned whole. A change to some of a piece's pages takes no sketch, since its other pages keep their design. A copy of another piece is drawn already, so it takes a sketch only where it is redesigned whole (DEC-003 point 8).
- **Another kind of work, on the person's word.** A piece of another kind, such as a figure or a poster, is designed by DEC-030 point 3's method from the start, unless the person asks for a sketch on that piece. It then starts with one, as a presentation does. A committed test, or what a person says of such pieces, is this record's trigger to make the sketch another kind's default (Alternatives, "The sketch as the default for every kind of work").
- **Skipped on the person's word.** The person may have the designer skip the sketch on any piece. The designer then designs that piece in the engine from the start (DEC-030 point 3). This record places neither the skip nor a sketch on another kind at any level of the delegation setting ([COR-072](../../../decisions/core/COR-072-delegation-levels.md) point 13). So no agent skips a sketch, or asks for one, in the person's place.

### 2. The sketch draws the piece's words as given, and the rebuild puts them in the content

- **The words come first.** Before the sketch, a new piece's words exist as the asker's text: the words the asker gives for the piece, in the language it is first written in. No content file need exist yet. For a whole piece redesigned, the words are its content's, in that language.
- **The takeaways.** The sketch reads the takeaways from the asker's text, where the asker states them. Before the content exists, nothing else holds one. For a whole piece redesigned, they are its pages' leads and its brief's takeaways ([visual-communication:DEC-029-piece-brief] point 2).
- **Drawn as given.** The sketch draws the words as given, and changes none of them. It draws each page so that it carries its takeaway.
- **Into the piece at the rebuild.** The rebuild takes the words from the asker's text, or from a redesigned piece's content, never from the sketch. It puts them in the piece's content, then in every language. It puts each takeaway where DEC-029 point 2 puts one: in the brief, for a page whose content states its point in no lead. So no word reaches the piece from the sketch alone. A word the sketch shows that neither holds is the designer's, within DEC-070 point 4.

### 3. The sketch is kept in the agent workspace, never committed

- **Where.** In the agent workspace of the checkout the designer works in, at `visual-communication/sketch/<piece>/` within it, a folder for each piece, named as the piece is. That is the capability's own folder there. The sketch lies nowhere in the capability's location, where the project's work lies ([visual-communication:DEC-002-files-and-validation] point 1), and no file of it is committed.
- **Who writes it.** The sketch session, as any agent writes in the agent workspace. So the designer's grant, which names the location alone, stays as it is (DEC-030 point 5).
- **How long.** It is kept at least until the person's ship call on the piece, so the person can set the piece beside it (point 6). A later sketch of the same piece replaces it. The workspace's removal command refuses a path under a capability's own folder, and a worktree that holds what another agent wrote is not removed without asking (the permissions README, "The agent workspace").
- **Who reads it.** The designer, to rebuild it, and the person. No command, check or review reads it. Once the rebuild is drawn, nothing draws from it: the piece's source is its content and its libraries (DEC-001 point 2).
- **Outside the capability's commands.** The sketch session draws the sketch, and the designer reads it, outside the commands, which draw and show only what the engine computes. The rebuilt piece is rendered, checked and looked at only through them.

### 4. The designer rebuilds the sketch at once, by its method

- **At once.** The designer rebuilds the sketch as soon as it is drawn. The person does not choose it first, and sees it at the ship call (point 6).
- **What the rebuild is.** The designer draws the sketch as the piece itself, in the engine: its content in every language, under the brand the piece names, with the libraries the project holds.
- **By the method.** The designer's method and its design knowledge govern the rebuild, save the choice of form, which the sketch has made (DEC-030 points 3 and 8). It writes the brief's goals and relations from what the asker says, and shows them to the asker before it designs against them (DEC-030 point 2).
- **What it keeps.** Each page and what it shows: the structure, the form and the visual variables that carry it, the hierarchy and the arrangement. The method names the structure each page shows before it carries the form, weighs another form, and says why it keeps the sketch's. It departs from the sketch's form only where that form bends the content's meaning (DEC-030 point 4), and says why.
- **What it takes from the brand.** For each choice of type, colour and space the sketch made, the brand's role that does that choice's job where the piece is shown. Examples are an accent where the sketch set one thing apart, and a size of text read across a room, for a piece shown in one. It writes no raw value, since only a brand holds one (DEC-001 point 5).
- **A notation's concepts.** Where a notation the piece names holds a concept that a part of the sketch shows, the rebuild maps the sketch's colour meanings to the notation's concepts. That part then takes its colour from its concept (DEC-001 point 6).
- **A library's component, an extension or a local one.** It uses a library's component where one draws a part of the sketch as well as the sketch does. Otherwise it takes one of DEC-030 point 4's ways: it extends a library through its owner's bar, or draws the part as a local component. Whether a component draws a part as well is the designer's judgement.
- **The reasons.** It writes the brief's reasons as it rebuilds, in the brief's order (DEC-030 point 3). They are the piece's reasons, those of the choices it keeps from the sketch among them.

### 5. What the rebuild cannot carry is drawn as a stand-in, and reaches its owner

- **The nearest that keeps the meaning, never a bend.** Where the brand, the engine or a library lacks what a part of the sketch needs, and no way of DEC-030 point 4 that the designer may take draws the part as the sketch does, the rebuild draws a stand-in: the nearest they allow that keeps the part's meaning. It never bends the meaning to fit (DEC-030 point 4). A difference or a relation the nearest would lose, such as one the sketch's colours carry, it carries by another means they allow. Where none keeps the meaning, it stops on that part, as DEC-030 point 4 says.
- **In a rebuild only.** A stand-in is drawn only where a piece is rebuilt from a sketch. In any other design, DEC-030 point 4's stop stands as it is.
- **What lacks it, in the terms that name it already:**
  - **a library or a brand the designer may not write,** such as a shipped library in a project or a library of another repository: a request to its owner, which the designer proposes to the person as a task (DEC-030 point 4). The stand-in is what that point's work goes on with meanwhile;
  - **the engine,** a tool it lacks: a need (DEC-070 point 5). It goes to the person as a question that names what the design needs, as DEC-030 point 4 has a stop do, and stops only the work that rests on it. The stand-in keeps the part's meaning, so the rest of the piece goes on.
- **In the brief's reasons.** The brief's reason for each part drawn as a stand-in names what lacks it and that lack's owner, beside the principle it rests on (DEC-029 point 2).
- **Its owner.** The owner is the project for its own libraries, whoever maintains the capability for the engine and the libraries the capability ships, and another repository's owner for a library of that repository. The owner judges a request or a need by the bar it keeps (DEC-030 point 4).
- **Libraries grow from below.** A library or a brand grows from what pieces ask of it: by an extension the designer makes through its owner's bar, and by a request or a need its owner takes, in a version of its own. A piece takes what was added when its lock moves (DEC-003 point 8). No count of requests or needs decides ([visual-communication:DEC-007-starter-library] point 1).

### 6. The round runs on the rebuilt piece, and the person sees the sketch beside it at the ship call

- **The round, as on any piece.** DEC-070's round runs on the rebuilt piece. It mends what DEC-070 point 3 names, and nothing else.
- **Nothing against the sketch.** The review is not handed the sketch, and reads the piece as DEC-070 point 5 says. A difference between the piece and the sketch is no finding, and the round mends nothing toward the sketch.
- **Side by side at the ship call.** With what DEC-070 point 2 hands over go where the sketch lies, the sketch session's account of what it was given and read, and each stand-in, with what lacks it, its owner and what was drawn in its place. So the person sets the sketch beside the piece. Whether the piece keeps what made the sketch good is the person's to judge.
- **Mends toward the sketch, on the person's word.** The person may ask for mends toward the sketch, as for any mend: the designer mends what the person names, and the review reads every page again (DEC-070 point 2).
- **Starting again.** A piece the person would have start again is a whole piece redesigned, which starts with a new sketch (point 1).

## Rationale

**Why a sketch first, drawn by a session of its own.** In both tests, what a fresh session drew outside the engine, with no guidance, reached the person's line. Nothing the capability drew in the engine did: its own drawing in the first test, and its designer's drawing by its method in the second. The first round of the tests' loop under a critic, drawn outside the engine too, did not ship, so drawing outside the engine is not alone what made the free drafts work. So this record takes whole the condition the tests found working: a fresh session, no guidance, no library and no brand, outside the engine. It claims nothing of which part of it worked. A library's components and a brand's values, met first, pull each page toward what they already draw, which is the loss the design agents were made to stop (DEC-030, Context). The rebuild then shows where the brand, the engine and the libraries fall short of what the content asked for.

**Why the sketch session has a definition of its own.** A session started with no definition holds every tool and the instructions the project loads, so it could read the design knowledge, the libraries and the brand: the guidance the condition keeps out. A definition of its own narrows its tools to what drawing the sketch needs, and its near-empty body brings in no method. Not every harness can narrow what a session reads to the files handed to it. There the isolation rests on the session's restraint, as the review's reading order does (DEC-070 point 5), and its account of what it read lets a breach show.

**Why presentations, and other kinds of work on the person's word.** Every sketch in the evidence was of a presentation that explains. Other kinds of work are read otherwise: a figure within a page of text, a poster whole and from afar, a site by scrolling. Nothing tested shows that what worked for presentations works for them. A default for every kind would carry a finding about presentations to every project's figures and posters, at the cost of a rebuild each time (COR-014). So the sketch is the default for presentations alone, and another kind keeps DEC-030 point 3's method, since no test weighed either way on it. The person may ask for a sketch on a piece of another kind, so a project that wants one has it piece by piece. What the person then says of those pieces, or a committed test, is what would make a kind's default. The cost is a piece of another kind that a sketch would have served, designed by the method unless the person asks.

**Why the person may skip it, and no agent in their place.** The person weighs a rebuild's cost against what a sketch may give on a piece. The tests found no judge but the person that held on whether a design worked (DEC-070, Rationale, "Why the person makes the ship call"), so nothing could check an agent's skip. So this record places the skip at no level.

**Why the method governs the rebuild, and not the sketch.** The method's worth is the order of its choices and the reasons a reviewer and a later designer rely on (DEC-030, "Why this record decides the method's order, and the body its steps"). Both are written at the rebuild, so the method governs where its worth lies. A sketch the method guided was never observed: every free draft that shipped was unguided, and the one guided drawing in the evidence did not ship. Guiding the sketch would try an untested condition on the person's pieces. The sketch is no piece, and no design of the designer's, so drawing it unguided leaves the method standing for every design the designer makes. Only its choice of form gives way, in a sketched piece, to the form the sketch drew.

**Why the rebuild runs at once, and the person sees the sketch at the ship call.** The person's time is what a piece spends most dearly, and the ship call already asks for it (DEC-070 point 1). A look before the rebuild would ask the person twice for one piece, and would judge a drawing the piece will never look like: in the test, a shipped free draft became a shipped piece once in two. Seen beside the piece at the ship call, the sketch shows the person what the rebuild kept and what it lost, in one look. A mend toward the sketch is then a mend the person asks for, which DEC-070 point 2 gives them already. The cost is a rebuild spent on a sketch the person would not have chosen. The person then asks for mends, or has the piece start again.

**Why no review reads the sketch.** The free drafts that reached the person's line had no review. The person ranked the polished rounds of the tests' loop under a critic below an earlier round of it (DEC-070, Context). The review also reads what the viewing step drew, with its frames and its report (DEC-070 point 5), and a sketch has none of them.

**Why the words come from the asker's text.** A piece's content chooses among what its libraries declare, its layouts and components among them (DEC-001 point 3), and the brief is keyed by the content's pages (DEC-029 point 3). Written before the sketch, the content would bring a library's structure in first, which the sketch is drawn to keep out. The asker's text holds the words before any of that. The sketch draws them as given, so from the rebuild on, the content is their one source, and DEC-070 point 4 says whose each word is.

**Why the sketch is kept in the agent workspace.** A sketch holds what the capability keeps out of a piece's files: literal positions, raw values and fonts (DEC-001 points 3 and 5). It also holds the piece's words in one language, which would drift from the content. Committed, it would be a second source of the design beside the logical layer (DEC-001 point 2). Its files would stay in history for good, as the files of an export nobody handed out would ([visual-communication:DEC-006-work-outside-the-engine-and-outputs], "Why in the piece's folder, and what is committed"). The agent workspace is never committed, and every agent may write there, so the sketch session needs no grant of its own and the designer's grant stays the location alone (DEC-030, "Why the grant names only the location"). Under the capability's own folder it lasts until the ship call, since the workspace's removal command does not reach it. The cost is that it lies in one checkout, apart from the pages the viewing step drew. So the designer names where it lies when it hands the piece over (point 6), and a person who wants it on another machine copies it.

**Why the rebuild keeps the form and takes the brand's values.** The rebuild keeps of the sketch what no brand gives: its structure, form, hierarchy and arrangement. Type, colour and space are a brand's to give (DEC-001 point 5), so the piece looks like the project's other pieces, and switching its brand restyles it. Taking the role that does each of the sketch's choices' job where the piece is shown keeps what those choices meant under the brand's look. A notation's concept keeps a meaning a colour carries the same in every piece that shows it (DEC-001 point 6). The rebuild has a measured cost: all six free drafts shipped, and one rebuild of two shipped. That is what a piece pays for one source in the logical layer and a brand it can switch (DEC-001 points 2 and 5).

**Why the nearest, never a bend, in a rebuild.** Part of what the second rebuild lost was the designer's. It dropped a meaning the sketch's colours carried, a bend DEC-030 point 4 forbids, and it drew running text at the screen's size for a room. The nearest that keeps the meaning, never a bend, answers the first (point 5), and the brand's role for where the piece is shown answers the second (point 4). A rebuild's form is fixed: the sketch drew it with no library in sight, and the rebuild keeps it. So a rebuild meets more of what the brand, the engine and the libraries lack than a design that chooses its own form, and one that stopped on each such part would bring the person no piece to judge. Drawn as a stand-in and reported, each lack reaches the owner who can meet it for every piece, with the piece that asked for it, and the person meets the piece with each loss in sight. A renderer reports what its medium could not draw in the same way (DEC-001 point 2). A design by the method meets fewer such gaps, and its stop brings each to the person as DEC-030 point 4 has it.

**Why a rebuild's lacks keep the names they have.** A lack in a library or a brand the designer may not write is already a request to its owner (DEC-030 point 4), and a tool the engine lacks is already a need (DEC-070 point 5). A rebuild's lack is of one of those two kinds, so it takes that kind's name, and each owner reads one kind of request however the piece started.

**Why the brief's reason names the lack and its owner.** The sketch is never committed, and what the person is handed at the ship call is a message. The brief is committed beside the piece, and its reason for a part is where a reviewer or a later designer looks for why the part is drawn so (DEC-029, Context). Naming there what was lacking and whose it is shows them what would let the part be drawn as the sketch drew it, once the owner meets the request or the need.

**Why DEC-030 point 4's ways stand.** The test extended no library and no brand, so it gives no reason to bar an extension through its owner's bar during a rebuild. That bar already keeps a shared library from a form shaped by one piece alone (DEC-030, "Why extend, make or stop, and never bend").

**Why the round mends nothing toward the sketch, and the review never reads it.** No test measured a model's reading of a rebuild against its sketch beside the person's. A difference from the sketch may be a loss or a gain, a judgement of taste, which the tests found the person alone made reliably (DEC-070, Rationale, "Why the round mends these, and leaves the rest"). A round that mended toward the sketch would be a round of polish, and the rounds of polish of the tests' loop under a critic ranked below an earlier round. The person sees the sketch beside the piece, and a mend they ask for mends what they name, as the round on the person's notes did in the test.

**Why a whole redesign, and not a change to some pages.** The tests drew whole presentations whose words already existed, which is a whole piece redesigned. A sketch of some pages, drawn free of the brand and the libraries, would be rebuilt beside pages it never saw, and no test drew one.

### Alternatives considered

- **For a presentation, the designer draws in the engine, with the brand, from the start,** as DEC-030 point 3 has it for every piece. Not taken: in the test, that drawing and its mended version did not reach the person's line. Its trigger to revisit: a person says of most presentations they judge, wherever the capability is installed, that the sketch did not help, or a committed test finds so.
- **The sketch as the default for every kind of work.** Not taken, for the reasons of "Why presentations, and other kinds of work on the person's word". Its trigger to revisit: a committed test finds rebuilt sketches of another kind reaching the person's line, or a person says of pieces of that kind they had sketched, wherever the capability is installed, that the sketch served them.
- **A sketch the method guides,** drawn by the designer, or by a session given the method and the design knowledge. Not taken: no such drawing was observed, and the one guided drawing in the evidence did not ship. Its trigger to revisit: a committed test shows a guided drawing reaching the person's line as often as a sketch.
- **A session with no definition of its own, given only the words.** Rejected: it would hold every tool and the project's loaded instructions, so its isolation would rest on restraint even where a harness could narrow what it reads.
- **The person chooses the sketch before the rebuild.** Not taken: the person would look twice at one piece, the first time at a drawing the piece will never look like, and a shipped free draft became a shipped piece once in two. Its trigger to revisit: a person says of pieces they judge, wherever the capability is installed, that a rebuild was often spent on a sketch they would not have had rebuilt, or a committed test finds so.
- **The person's look as a ship call on the sketch.** Rejected: the sketch reaches no audience, and the rebuild may lose or gain what the person judged.
- **A review of the sketch.** Rejected: no review read the free drafts that reached the person's line, and the review reads what the viewing step drew.
- **Several sketches, for the person to choose among.** Not taken: one reached the person's line in each test, and the person may have a piece start again. Its trigger to revisit: a person, wherever the capability is installed, often has a piece start again from a new sketch, or a committed test finds that one sketch of several reaches the person's line more often than one alone.
- **The sketch committed in the piece's folder.** Rejected, for the reasons of "Why the sketch is kept in the agent workspace".
- **The sketch kept in the clone's local folder, beside the viewing step's images.** Rejected: that folder lies in the methodology's own folder and holds only what commands write (DEC-002 point 1), so a session's writes there would need a grant DEC-030 point 5 keeps out of it, and the viewing step replaces what it draws there on each run.
- **The nearest that keeps the meaning in every design, in place of DEC-030 point 4's stop.** Not taken: a design that chooses its own form meets fewer gaps, and nothing tested a stand-in there. Its trigger to revisit: a person says of pieces designed by the method, wherever the capability is installed, that a stop held work a stand-in would have served, or a committed test finds so.
