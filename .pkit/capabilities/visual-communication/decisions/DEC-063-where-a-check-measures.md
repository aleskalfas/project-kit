**In plain terms:** a check that names nothing at a place has not always measured it. It may never compare that kind of place: the checks of boxes compare only the parts one group holds directly, so they say nothing of a title in one group beside a paragraph in another. Or it may measure nothing there: the check of edges that nearly line up finds nothing near where a brand gives no step of space. A reader that takes such a silence for "clean" skips a sum it should make. So a check whose findings come from computing a scene, and whose silence a reader leans on, states which kinds of place it measures and which it leaves out, its *reach*, in its entry in the capability's list of checks and, in the same words, in its code, beside an id of its own. It does so in the change that teaches a reader to read its silence, or, where a reader already reads it, in the change that lands the check. Any other such check may state one. Where a check with a reach could measure nothing at a place of a kind it measures, it states that in the piece, as a fact. A check's silence at a place then counts as measured and clean only where the place is of a kind the check measures and no such statement covers it. A check that states no reach counts as having measured nothing, though its findings stand. Everywhere else the check did not measure, and the reviewer judges the place as the records of its reading say. Siblings a check of boxes did not measure, it reads from the frames. The kinds of place their reach leaves out, it judges on the image first, and reads the frames there where it sees a near miss or an overlap. An image with no frames, it judges on the image alone, and says so. A check measures and never judges: judging is the reviewer's, and the other readers'. Where the reviewer reads the findings and each check's reach is [visual-communication:DEC-062-checks-beside-the-frames]'s.

## Context

[visual-communication:DEC-046-faults-lower-grades] point 3 adds two checks that measure the boxes of siblings, edges that nearly line up and boxes that overlap. Where they ran, the reviewer does no sums over the frames, and where they did not, it reads the offsets from the frames. Those sentences do not tell apart, in a scene the checks ran on, a place they measured from one they do not compare or could not measure. Point 5 of the same record has a check of a text's size state, as a fact, where it measured no text's size: once in its entry for a kind of text it never measures, and in the piece for a text it meets and cannot measure. No record says the same of any other check, nor reads a check's silence place by place.

A check that ran on a scene can be silent at a place it did not measure, in three ways:
- **A kind of place it never compares.** A header group holds a title, and a body group holds a paragraph. The groups' left edges agree, the title is set 0 px in and the paragraph 3 px, under a brand whose smallest step of space is 4 px. The checks of boxes compare the two groups, and the parts each holds only with each other, so they never compare the title with the paragraph. A line that stops 2 px short of the part it joins is passed over too, since the checks leave out lines.
- **A line its own rule passes over.** The check of edges that nearly line up does not report a near miss on one line of an axis where the pair lines up on another line of it (DEC-046 point 3). Two cards in a row whose tops agree, 200 and 203 px tall, are not reported at their bottoms.
- **A place it could not measure.** The same check finds nothing near at a key whose brand gives no step of space, since it measures nearness against that step.

A reader that took any of these silences for "measured and clean" would do no sums there, and pass an offset: the craft the first calibration found the review weak on (DEC-046, Context).

The words it uses:
- **A check** is a measurement a command makes on every render, with no judgement. Its finding fails, asks for attention and names who is to act, or states a fact, which asks no act ([visual-communication:DEC-002-files-and-validation] point 7). So this record says of a check only what it measures, and keeps *judge* for the reviewer and the other readers of its findings.
- **The capability's list of checks** is its page that lists each check and its findings. Each of its rows is one check: the row is the check's *entry*, and its *labels* are the names its findings carry. A row may carry several labels, and one label may stand in several rows: a role with no value takes one label whether a formula, a text's style or an image reads it.
- **A scene** is a piece's pages as the engine lays them out, in one language and at one combination of its sizes, which a *key* names with its modes ([visual-communication:DEC-004-brands-and-notations] point 3).
- **A place** is what a finding names in one scene: an element, a pair or group of elements, or content.
- **A scene check** is a check whose findings come from computing a scene, whatever they point at: the check of content a size hides points at the content's key path. Checks of files, of a lock, of content against the components it fills, of the brief or of an export compute no scene, and this record does not bind them.
- **A check of colour** is a scene check that measures a scene in each combination of modes it serves, such as light and dark. Each of its findings names the combinations it holds for, or names none and holds for every combination its scene serves, as a sign that paints neither a fill nor a stroke shows nothing in any.
- **A kind of place** is a class of places an entry can name, such as two siblings, two parts of different groups, a line, or a text inside a picture.
- **A check's reach** is the kinds of place it measures, and the kinds it leaves out.
- **A reader of the checks** is whoever reads what a check found so as to leave to it what it measures. The reviewer is one: the capability's agent that judges the drawn pages and reports what no check measures ([visual-communication:DEC-015-viewing-step-and-reviewer] point 7).
- **Siblings** are the parts one part holds directly. *Holds*, said of a part in this record, is said only of the parts it holds directly, never of one box inside another. **A part inside a sibling's box** is one of two siblings whose box lies within the other's, as a card's head lies within the box drawn behind it. **The frames** are the file beside each image of the viewing step that gives every drawn part's box (DEC-046, Context).

## Decision

**A scene check whose silence a reader leans on states its reach, in its entry and in its code, and any other may. A check with a reach states as a fact where it could measure nothing at a place of a kind it measures. A check's silence at a place counts as measured and clean only where both allow, and a check that states no reach counts as having measured nothing. At every other place it did not measure, and the reviewer judges there as the records of its reading say.**

### 1. A scene check a reader leans on states its reach

- **What it measures, and what it leaves out.** A scene check's reach names the kinds of place it measures and the kinds it leaves out.
- **What its own rule passes over is left out.** A kind of place the check never reports, by a rule of its own, counts among what it leaves out, and its reach names it.
- **Every path its code passes over.** Each way the check's code can pass over a place of a kind it measures, giving no finding, is a clause of its reach, worded as exactly as the code passes over it, or a statement of point 2 where it happens, or a departure. A path *departs* where it passes over a place that the check's record has it measure. The work that finds a departure fixes it, or files the fix as a follow-up and states the path meanwhile as a clause, worded as the code passes over it.
- **Who decides a clause.** The record that decides a check decides its reach, and this point has the entry and the code state it. A clause that says the check measures a kind of place comes only from that check's record. A clause that leaves a kind out cites the record that decides it. Where no record decides it, the clause may still be stated, citing this point, since a kind left out fails safe: its places go to the reader's own judgement (point 3). A kind is decided by no record only where the words of the check's record neither reach it nor leave it out. Where they reach it, a path that passes over it is a departure. A clause stated for a departure cites the record the code departs from.
- **Once, in the entry.** A kind of place the check leaves out in every piece is stated there alone, never in each piece, as DEC-046 point 5 has the check of a text's size state a kind of text it never measures.
- **In its code, in the entry's words, beside an id.** The check's code states its reach in the words of its entry, beside an id of the check's own that stays as the check changes. The entry shows that id, and a test, joining entry and code on it, holds the two texts equal. So whoever reads what the code found can read with it the reach of the commit that ran, and tell apart two checks whose findings share a label. A test for each clause of the reach plants a place of its kind, with a fault there: where the reach measures that kind, the check reports the fault; where the reach leaves it out, the check gives no finding; where a statement covers it, the check states so. So a clause the code does not keep fails a test. No test fails on a path a later change adds that no clause names, so a change that alters a check with a reach repeats the audit of every path its code passes over. Whoever reviews that change holds it to the audit, reading the entry against the code, which states the reach beside the check the change alters.
- **Two duties of every scene check,** whether or not it states a reach. It gives its findings scene by scene, each tagged with its scene's language and key, and with its page where its place lies on one, and leaves any merging of findings across scenes to the render's own code. A finding that holds alike in every language, or at every key, may say so in place of naming one. And it states in its code the axes it compares scenes across: the sizes, the languages, or both. A check compares scenes where its finding in one scene depends on another, and one that reads several scenes only to give a finding once compares none, and says so. So a reader can place each finding, and each silence, in its scene (point 3), and whatever runs the check knows which scenes to compute.
- **No reach, no measurement.** A scene check that states no reach counts as having measured no place, whatever it found. Its findings still reach every reader, and only its silence counts for nothing.
- **A reach comes with the reading that needs it.** The checks a reader leans on are exactly those whose silence the reader's instructions read: for the reviewer, those whose silence its body reads. The change that teaches a reader to read a check's silence writes that check's reach. A check whose silence a reader's instructions already read when the check lands states its reach in the change that lands it. Any other scene check may gain one, and need not.

### 2. Where it could measure nothing, it states so, as a fact

- **At a place of a kind it measures.** A scene check that could measure nothing at such a place states so in the piece, with the reason. The check of edges that nearly line up does so at a key whose brand gives no step of space, or one no greater than a pixel of rounding, since nothing can then be near. DEC-046 point 5 has the check of a text's size do so for a text whose size or minimum it cannot work out. This point has every scene check that states a reach do so, whichever record decides it.
- **What a statement covers.** A statement names how far it reaches: one place, a page, a key or the scene. It covers each place there of a kind the check measures. So the check of edges that nearly line up states it once at each such key, and covers every pair and group of siblings at that key.
- **A fact.** It asks no act of the project, since nothing in the piece's files is wrong: a brand may give no step of space by choice. A reader that relies on the check judges the places it covers itself, as part of its own reading, which no finding asks of it (DEC-002 point 7). So it fails nothing, and names no one to act.
- **A finding of its check, like any other.** The check's code states it, wherever that code runs. A render keeps it in the piece's findings record, and validation shows it as it shows each finding that record holds while its key verifies ([visual-communication:DEC-005-engine-and-plugins] point 13). A findings record a release wrote before its check made the statement holds none of it, so validation shows none until the piece's next render. Where each reader reads the statement is the record of that reader's reading.

### 3. How a check's silence is read

Per check, and per place:
- **Measured and clean:** a place of a kind the check's reach measures, in a scene the check ran on, where the check gave no finding and no statement of its covers the place. A check of colour measured such a place, and found it clean, only in each combination of modes it measured there and found nothing in. A place is measured only for what that check measures: a pair of siblings the check of boxes that overlap measured is measured for overlap, not for its edges.
- **Measured, with a finding:** a place of a kind the check's reach measures, in a scene the check ran on, where the check gave a finding. The finding stands for what it names, and what else the check measures at that place it measured and found clean, so a pair of siblings with a near miss across is measured and clean down. A check of colour measured such a place with a finding in each combination its finding names, or in every combination its scene serves where it names none.
- **Not measured:** every other place. That takes in a kind of place the reach leaves out, a place a statement covers, every place in a scene the check did not run on, and every place of a check that states no reach. For a check of colour, it takes in each combination it did not measure, such as a browser's forced palette.
- **By the reach the code that ran states.** A reader reads each check's reach as the code that measured the place states it, since the reach may change from one release, or one commit, to the next. Where a reader finds that reach beside the findings is the record of that reader's reading.

### 4. Where a check did not measure, the reviewer judges as the records of its reading say

- **The checks of boxes.** Where one of them did not measure a place of a kind its reach measures, the reviewer reads from the frames what that check measures. At a kind of place its reach leaves out, the reviewer judges on the image first, and reads the frames there only where the image shows a near miss or an overlap. On an image the check did not measure, or whose report from the viewing step carries no reach for it, the reviewer reads siblings from the frames, and judges every other place on the image first. On an image with no frames, it judges on the image alone, and the review's report says so. DEC-046 point 3 says this, in the words this record's Implications give it.
- **A check of a text's size.** Where it measured no text's size, the review's report names the gap (DEC-046 point 5).
- **Every other check.** What it measures is the reviewer's to judge at that place, as is everything no check measures (DEC-015 point 7). That takes in every place of a check that states no reach.
- **A passing check is one that measured.** A drawing disagrees with a passing check only at a place the check measured and found clean ([visual-communication:DEC-030-design-agents] point 6). Where the check did not measure the place, a fault the page shows there is of its own kind, and blames no check.
- **The same grade either way.** Whether the reviewer names a defect as a drawing that disagrees with a passing check or as a fault of its own kind, the defect is one, so its fault cites the rule whose words name that defect (DEC-046 point 2). A check's reach moves no grade.

## Rationale

**Why silence is read per place and per check.** A check that ran on a scene names nothing at a place it never compares, nor at one it could not measure. Read per scene, its silence says that the title and the paragraph of two groups were compared, when they were not (Context). Read per place but not per check, a key where the check of edges that nearly line up measured nothing would lose the overlaps the check of boxes that overlap still measured there. Only the place and the check together say what was measured.

**Why a check of colour is read per combination.** A check of contrast measures a scene in each combination it serves, and each of its findings names one. A text it finds faint in light, it measured and found clean in dark. Read per scene, its finding in light would leave the text measured and not clean on the dark image too, so a fade drawn there would blame no check. Read per combination, the image of each key is read by what was measured at that key, and a browser's forced palette, which the check never measured, by nothing.

**Why the entry states the reach.** The reach is the check's, and changes when its code changes. Written once beside the check, it stays one statement, in step with the check, that every reader reads alike. A reader that kept its own account of each check would drift from it.

**Why the code states it too, beside an id, with a test and planted cases.** A reader reads what the code found, so it needs the reach of the code that ran, and only the code can carry it wherever its findings go, at any release and any commit. One label may stand in several rows, so a label cannot say whose reach a silence is read by. An id of the check's own, kept beside its reach, can. It stays as the check changes, so that a later comparison across versions can match a check from one version to the next. A test holds the reach's text equal to the entry, which shows the id the two are joined on. It holds only the words, though: a check's code and its entry may both speak of a text over a picture, while the code leaves out a whole text where one part of it lies over one. A planted case for each clause holds the code to its words.

**Why every path the code passes over is in the reach.** A path that gives no finding, and that nothing names, reads as measured and clean. The check of edges that nearly line up finds nothing near at a step no greater than a pixel of rounding, as at no step. A check of contrast may pass over a combination in which it finds no ground under a run, or no ink for it. Each such path is either a kind of place, which the reach names once, or a place of one piece, which a statement names where it happens (point 2). A path that passes over what the check's record has it measure is no reach that record decides, but an error of the code. Made a clause and nothing more, it would settle how the record is read without the record. So it is fixed, or filed and stated as a clause meanwhile, since until then a silence nothing names would read as clean.

**Why a clause that leaves a kind out needs no record, and one that measures does.** A kind left out sends its places to the reader's own judgement, which costs sums and errs safe, for the reason below. So a path no record decides may be named as left out at once. A clause that says a check measures a kind lets a reader skip its own judgement there, so it must rest on what the check's record decides the check does.

**Why a check that states no reach counts as having measured nothing.** It fails safe. A silence read as measured where the check did not measure costs a missed defect. One read as not measured where it did costs the reader its own judgement there: sums, or, for content a size hides, a reading of the piece's components that may name a part hidden on purpose as missing. With no reach stated, nothing tells a reader what a check's silence covers, so only the second error is safe: a part named missing in error is seen, and a missed defect is not. Every check still gives its findings, so this decides only which silences count.

**Why a reach comes with the reading that needs it.** A check whose silence no reader reads costs no one anything without a reach: its findings reach every reader, and its silence is read as not measured. A reach owed by every change to any scene check would ask reaches no reader needs, and nothing in such a change would show whoever reviews it the duty, since a check with no reach states none beside its code. A change to a check with a reach does show it: the reach stands in the code the change alters. The change that teaches a reader to read a check's silence is the one that needs the reach, and the one a review of that change can hold to it. Where the reading comes first, as DEC-046 point 5 has the reviewer's body word its reading of a check of a text's size before the check ships, the change that lands the check is the one that needs it, and a review of that change can hold it to the reading already there.

**Why every scene check gives its findings scene by scene, and states the axes it compares across.** Silence is read per scene (point 3), so a reader needs the scene each finding holds in, and the scenes a finding in one depends on: where one of those could not be computed, the check measured nothing in the scenes it compares with it. Stated in the check's code, the axes tell whatever runs it which scenes to compute, and a check that reads several scenes only to give a finding once asks for no scene more. A check that merged its own findings across scenes would give one finding for many places, and leave the rest looking measured and clean. A finding that says it holds in every language, or at every key, leaves no scene looking clean. Merging for the findings record stays the render's, in one place for every check.

**Why what a check's own rule passes over counts as left out.** DEC-046 point 3 has the check of edges that nearly line up pass over a near miss that follows from the line chosen, so that an icon and a label centred in a row are not called near at their tops. Two cards whose tops agree and whose heights differ by 3 px are passed over by the same rule, yet whether they are a fault is a judgement on the image. Read as left out, those lines go to the reader, who judges them, rather than to a silence that seems to pass them.

**Why a place it could not measure is stated in the piece.** Whether a brand gives a step of space at a key is the piece's brand's, and differs from piece to piece and key to key, so no entry could state it. Only the check knows where it measured nothing, so it states it where that happens. A kind of place it leaves out in every piece is stated once, in its entry, so that no piece carries a fact for it (DEC-046, "Why the check says where it judged no size").

**Why a fact, and not a call for attention.** A call for attention expects someone to act, and names who (DEC-002 point 7). Nothing in the piece is to mend: a brand that gives no step of space may mean it. A call for attention would ask the piece's author to act on every piece drawn under such a brand, for ever. The reader judges the places it covers as part of its reading, as it does wherever no check measures.

**Why siblings from the frames, and the rest on the image first.** Siblings are the places the checks of boxes sum. Where a check did not sum them, DEC-046 point 3 has the reviewer read the offsets from the frames, since the first calibration found the review judging alignment by eye, though the frames give each box (DEC-046, "Why craft gains none"). The kinds the checks' reach leaves out take in every pair of parts in different groups, and, for every pair that lines up on one line of an axis, the other lines of it: most pairs on a page. No reader sums over them all, and DEC-046 point 3 asks no sums at a kind it leaves out: lines and arrows, "whose crossings stay the reviewer's to find". A reviewer that judges the image first there, as it judges what no check measures, does the sums the image sends it to. A near miss too small to see goes unsummed there, until widening the check to edges across groups makes the commonest of those places the check's (Implications). On an image the checks did not measure, the same line divides the places, since siblings are still those the checks sum. An image with no frames leaves nothing to sum, so the reviewer judges it on the image alone, and says so, since nothing unseen reads as clean (DEC-015 point 7).

**Why every scene check, in a record of its own.** How a check states its reach and how its silence is read (points 1 to 3) hold for every scene check a reader leans on, not only the two of boxes and the one of a text's size, and for every reader, wherever it reads the findings. Each later check's record rests on them, and looks for them under what it decides, not under the record of the viewing step. A check that computes no scene has no silence at a place in one to read, so this record leaves it out. Point 4 is the reviewer's alone, since the reviewer is the reader the capability ships. DEC-062 decides where the reviewer reads the findings and each check's reach. This record decides how a reader reads them.

**Why validation shows the statement too.** It is the check's own finding, made by the code a render runs. A person who runs the check sees where it measured nothing, as the reviewer does. A record written before a check made the statement holds none of it, so the two may differ until the next render, and each shows what its own run found.

### What it costs

- **A reach for each scene check a reader leans on,** in its entry and in its code, beside its id, with a test that holds the two equal, a planted case for each clause, and an audit of each path its code passes over, which each change that alters the check repeats, with a fix or a follow-up for each path that departs from its record. No other scene check pays it until a reading needs it.
- **Findings scene by scene,** from every scene check, and the axes each compares across, stated in its code.
- **The reader's own judgement,** at each kind of place a reach leaves out, each place a statement covers, and every place of a check with no reach. Where the reviewer judges the image first, a near miss it does not see there is passed.

### Alternatives considered

- **Silence read per scene,** so that a check that ran on a scene counts as having measured every place in it. Rejected: it reads the title and the paragraph of two groups as compared (Context).
- **The silence of a check of colour read per scene.** Rejected, for the reason above: a fade on the image of one key would blame no check.
- **The check of edges that nearly line up measures every line of an axis.** Not taken: DEC-046 point 3 decides that a near miss which follows from the line chosen is not reported. The lines it passes over go to the reader instead (point 1).
- **A call for attention where a check could measure nothing.** Rejected, for the reason above.
- **Each kind of place a check leaves out stated as a fact in every piece.** Rejected: it would repeat a fact of the check in every piece, as DEC-046 point 5 found for a kind of text the check of a text's size never measures.
- **The reach kept in the reader's own instructions,** such as the reviewer's body. Rejected: the reach is the check's, and changes with its code, so a copy elsewhere would drift.
- **A label as the check's name in code.** Rejected: one label may stand in several rows, so two checks would share one name.
- **A reach for every scene check before any counts.** Rejected. It asks a reach of every scene check, each in its entry and its code, before a reader may lean on one, so the checks the reviewer leans on would wait for the rest. Read as measured by none, a check with no reach costs only the reader's own judgement.
- **A reach for each scene check with the work that next changes it.** Rejected, for the reason above: it asks reaches no reader needs, and nothing in such a change shows whoever reviews it the duty.
- **A clause that a check measures a kind of place, stated where no record decides it.** Rejected, for the reason above: a reader skips its own judgement wherever a check measures.
- **The viewing step copies each reach from the installed list of checks when it runs.** Not taken. It needs one statement and no test of equality, but a page written for people would become an input each run reads, and its form would bind as an interface.
- **For the checks of boxes, the report lists the groups each compared on each image.** Not taken. It makes the places measured data rather than words, for two checks alone, and the kinds they leave out would still need stating.
- **The reviewer sums the frames at every kind of place the checks of boxes leave out.** Rejected: that is most pairs on a page, more than any reader sums.
- **The reviewer judges the image first wherever a check of boxes did not measure, siblings among them.** Rejected: siblings are the places the checks sum, and where they did not, DEC-046 point 3 has the reviewer read the frames, since judging alignment by eye is what the first calibration found weak.
- **These points in DEC-062.** Rejected: points 1 to 3 hold for every scene check and every reader, whatever DEC-062 decides of the viewing step, and a later check's record must find them. Point 4 is the reviewer's alone, but it reads a silence as points 1 to 3 define it, so it stays beside them.
