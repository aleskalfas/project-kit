**In plain terms:** the review of a piece is tested before its verdict may end the designer's loop. One check changes what a grade takes in. Where the check of a text's size measured a text, a text too small lowers no grade. Where it did not, the review judges that text by eye. So the review runs in two states, and a test in one says nothing of the other.

- **The test runs in the measured state.** The check measures every image of its designs, and each design passes the check.
- **The verdict ends a loop only on a piece in that state.** Elsewhere it informs. Where the command that gives the verdict cannot tell, it informs too.
- **Where a piece is shown is the asker's to say.** The designer never changes that statement to clear a finding without asking.
- **The checks of boxes change no grade's content,** so they narrow nothing.

## Context

The verdict ends the designer's loop only where an accepted calibration reaches ([visual-communication:DEC-042-grades-bar-and-loop] points 9 and 10). Four things the accepted records say bear on how far that is:

- **The hold's reach reads no piece.** It reaches any project whose bar covers only rules with their own evidence, while nothing lapses it (DEC-042 point 10). Every condition is the project's.
- **A calibration waits for each check the reviewer's body is written to read.** Otherwise the verdict would end loops "for a review that does not run as it was measured" ([visual-communication:DEC-046-faults-lower-grades] point 8).
- **A check that has landed has not measured every piece.** A check's silence counts only where it measured ([visual-communication:DEC-063-where-a-check-measures] point 3). The check of a text's size measures a piece only where the piece names its media ([visual-communication:DEC-055-smallest-text-size] point 9). Every piece written before that check names none.
- **No agent reaches a switch of the hold.** No agent reads the bar, which lies in configuration the project reviews (DEC-042 point 3). Only the change that records an accepted calibration writes the calibrated data (DEC-042 point 9).

What a check's not measuring does to a grade differs by check:

- **The check of a text's size.** Where it measured, a text under its medium's minimum is the check's finding alone, and lowers no grade of `RS-GRAPHIC-005` (DEC-046 point 5, "No small text fails twice"). Where it did not, the review judges a text's size as far as the image shows.
- **The two checks of boxes.** Where one did not measure a place of a kind it measures, the reviewer reads from the frames what that check measures (DEC-063 point 4). The defect holds its rule's grade either way (DEC-063 point 4, "The same grade either way").

The command that gives the verdict reads no check. It gives the hold to any bar within the accepted report. It names three reasons that the hold is off: no report accepted, a bar that reaches past it, and a lapse.

Terms:

- The *hold*, a *bar*, a *calibration*, its *held-out ladder*, the *verdict* and the *asker* are DEC-042's terms.
- A check *measured* a place, its *reach* and its *statement* are DEC-063's terms.
- A piece's *media* are the media it names that it is shown in (DEC-055 point 1).
- A check *takes a defect from a rule's grades* where an accepted record decides that a defect the check finds lowers no grade of that rule.

## Decision

**A check that takes a defect from a rule's grades narrows the hold, and no other check does. Under a bar that covers such a rule, the verdict ends a loop only on a piece that check measured, read by the review as the check measured it. Where the command cannot tell, the verdict informs. A calibration's ladder is measured by each such check, and each of its designs passes it. Where a piece is shown is the asker's statement, which the designer changes only on the asker's word.**

### 1. A check that takes a defect from a rule's grades narrows the hold, and no other does

- **Which checks.** A check narrows the hold where it takes a defect from the grades of a rule a bar covers. The review then grades that rule in two states: beside the check, where it measured, and without it.
- **The first.** The check of a text's size takes a text under its medium's minimum from the grades of `RS-GRAPHIC-005` (DEC-046 point 5). No other check takes a defect from a rule's grades.
- **The checks of boxes take none.** A defect at a place they name holds its rule's grade, as one the reviewer finds from the frames does (DEC-063 point 4). So a place they did not measure narrows nothing.
- **A later check.** The record that has a check take a defect from a rule's grades names this point. It says what it is for that check to have measured an image, as DEC-055 point 13 does.

### 2. The hold reaches a piece only where each such check measured it, as the review read it

Take a project's bar that covers a rule a check takes a defect from. The verdict then ends the designer's loop on a piece only where both of these hold of the round's review:

- **The check measured each image the review read.** The viewing step's report names the check for the image, and gives the image no statement of that check ([visual-communication:DEC-062-checks-beside-the-frames] point 2 and DEC-063 point 3).
- **The review read each image as the check measured it.** It left out nothing the piece states that the check measured by. The review leaves a piece's media out where they disagree with its `shown` field (DEC-055 point 12), and the hold then does not reach that round.

What follows from the two:

- **Elsewhere the verdict informs,** and the loop ends as [visual-communication:DEC-030-design-agents] point 2 says.
- **Where the command cannot tell, the verdict informs.** The command says so among its reasons that the hold is off. That holds wherever the data it would read is missing, cannot be read or is of another round. It holds under every such bar while the command reads no such data (point 6).
- **A bar that covers no such rule** is reached as DEC-042 point 10 says, with nothing added.
- **Read on each piece, at each round.** The hold's other conditions are the project's. This one is the piece's, at the round the verdict is given for. A piece gains it by naming its media, and a round loses it where its review read an image the check did not measure.

### 3. A calibration's ladder is measured by each such check, and each design passes it

A calibration whose bar covers such a rule fixes this of its held-out ladder before it runs, as it fixes the rest (DEC-042 point 9):

- **Measured.** Each such check measures every image of every design, and states for none that it could measure nothing.
- **Each design passes the check.** A loop's review reads no page such a check fails, since the designer mends a check's failure first (DEC-042 point 6). A ladder's designs are fixed, and nobody mends one. So each is drawn to pass, and the outside judge ranks the designs as they then stand.
- **Its report names what each such check measured by,** such as the ladder's media and the smallest size of each (DEC-055 point 13).
- **A ladder that cannot be measured.** A calibration that cannot fix such a ladder leaves the rule out of its bar, or waits. An example is a ladder shown in a medium whose smallest size no top declares.

### 4. Where a piece is shown is the asker's statement

This point holds wherever the designer mends a piece, under a bar or not, as DEC-042 point 7 does.

- **The asker's.** A piece's `shown` field and its media say where it is shown, and are the asker's statement. The designer writes them from what the asker says. It shows them to the asker as it shows the goals and relations (DEC-030 point 2).
- **Changed only on the asker's word.** A fix that would change either is asked of the person, as a fix that would change the asker's words is (DEC-042 point 7). The designer says what it recommends.
- **What stays the designer's.** It changes alone what the piece is drawn with, such as a style, a brand of the project's own, or a size the piece is drawn at. Where the piece maps its media, a size it adds reaches a medium only once the asker says that medium shows it.
- **Kept by the designer's own rule.** No command can tell whose word changed a field of the piece's file. The designer tells the person each change it made to either, as it tells every change (DEC-030 point 2).

### 5. What such a check measures against lapses no hold

- **The values are not compared.** A value such a check measures against, such as a top's smallest size, is not compared with the accepted data, and lapses nothing. It decides which pages reach the review, and never how the review reads one. A brand's type scale does as much.
- **Measured and clean moves no grade** (DEC-063 point 4, "The same grade either way").
- **The check's code, its reach and its statements** lapse the hold as every check's do (DEC-062, Implications, "What lapses the hold").
- **What the narrowing does not guard against.** It guards against no comparison at all, and against a review that read the piece otherwise than the check measured it. Three cases still get the hold:
  - a piece measured against a poor value, such as one a project's own top declares;
  - a piece whose `shown` field and media agree, and are both wrong;
  - a piece shown in a room farther than a room's value assumes (DEC-055 point 8).

  Whether a text can be read where the piece is shown stays the review's to grade in each.

### 6. The command comes to tell from data a later record decides

- **With the first such check, a reason and no data.** The change that lands a check that narrows the hold gives the command its reason of point 2. Until the command can tell, that reason holds under every bar that covers the check's rule.
- **Then the command comes to tell.** A follow-up has it read whether both conditions of point 2 hold of the round ([project-kit#FOLLOWUP]). The reason then holds only where one fails, or where the command cannot tell.
- **What the data must be.** Three things bind whichever source the follow-up takes:
  - **Of the round.** It describes the images that round's review read. A file a later look replaces does not, once it is replaced.
  - **Given again.** Anyone gives the verdict again from the same report (DEC-042 point 1), so the data stays with the round.
  - **Not the designer's.** No file the designer may write decides it (DEC-030 point 5).
- **A record decides the source,** as a refinement of this point. Two candidates stand. The round's kept report could carry it, written by the reviewer. Or the command could read a kept copy of the viewing step's report, which no check reads today (DEC-062 point 3).
- **No order binds the follow-up against a calibration.** The reason keeps the command safe either way.

## Rationale

**Why the hold reaches only a piece measured so.** The verdict ends loops by what a calibration measured (DEC-042 points 9 and 10). A calibration measures the review beside a check that takes every small text. On a piece that names no medium, nothing takes them, and the review says only what the image shows. The verdict would end a loop there for a review that does not run as it was measured (DEC-046 point 8).

- **It fails safe.** A verdict that informs is safe, as a lapse is (DEC-042 point 9).
- **What a project does.** A project that wants the verdict to end its loops says where its pieces are shown.

**Why by what a grade takes in, and not by every check the reviewer reads.** The two kinds of check leave the review in different places where they did not measure:

- **A check that takes a defect** leaves the grade with other content. Measured, a small text is outside the grade. Unmeasured, the review grades it, and only as far as an image shows (DEC-046 point 5). A calibration in one state gives no evidence of the grade in the other.
- **A check of boxes** leaves the grade as it was. The reviewer reads from the frames what the check would have summed, and the defect lowers the same grade (DEC-063 point 4). Only who does the arithmetic differs.
- **Its unmeasured state is rare.** A check of boxes measures every piece, but for a key whose brand gives no step of space, or a scene its code stopped on. The check of a text's size measures no piece until the piece names its media.
- **What this leaves untested.** A calibration does not test the reviewer's own sums from the frames. The Implications name when to weigh that again.

**Why one root is named.** Three things follow from a check whose finding lowers no grade. Each design of a ladder must pass it. The hold narrows by piece. A finding stands where a statement also covers its place (DEC-055 point 12). A later consequence of that choice of DEC-046 point 5 is recognised as one by the term this record defines.

**Why a record of its own.** The rule reaches every check that takes a defect from a rule's grades, whichever record decides the check. Two pieces of work look for it under the hold. One has the command compare the checks the review reads, and the other has it tell whether a check measured. Neither would look in the record of one check's sizes and media.

**Why the review must have read the piece as the check measured it.** Take a deck whose `shown` says it is projected, and whose media name a screen at hand alone. The check measures every run against the screen, and states nothing. The review leaves the media out, and judges the room as far as the image shows (DEC-055 point 12). That is the unmeasured state, on a piece every image of which the check measured.

**Why the command informs wherever it cannot tell.** The reason needs no data: the bar covers the rule, and the command cannot tell. So the hold is narrowed from the day the check lands, and no order of later work has to keep it. Once the command reads its data, the same sentence covers data that is missing or of another round. So the accepted text needs no second edit when the follow-up lands.

**Why each design of the ladder passes the check.** In the measured state, a text under its minimum lowers no grade, and the verdict reads grades alone (DEC-042 point 1). The outside judge ranks by the rules the bar covers, and would rank such a design down. The review could not. So the conditions of DEC-042 point 9 could fail for no fault of the review.

**Why where a piece is shown is the asker's.** DEC-042 keeps every switch of the hold from an agent. A piece's media lie in its own file, which the designer writes (DEC-030 point 5). Changing them changes what the check measures, what the review reads and whether the hold is on. So the designer could clear a failing check, and gain the hold, by saying the piece is shown elsewhere.

- **It is the asker's in fact.** Where a piece is shown is known to whoever asked for it, as its intent is (DEC-030 point 2).
- **`shown` beside the media.** The review judges by `shown` where the two disagree (DEC-055 point 12). A designer free to change `shown` alone would steer the review as one free to change the media steers the check.
- **Why no command holds it.** The piece's file is the designer's to write, and a first draft needs the field written. The asker's words are kept the same way, by the designer's own rule (DEC-042 point 7).

**Why the values such a check measures against lapse no hold.** DEC-042 point 9 lapses the hold on what could move the grades, compared with the accepted data. A smallest size moves none:

- **It decides which pages reach the review.** Under the hold, a text under it fails, and is mended before any review (DEC-042 point 6). How the review reads a page that reaches it stays as it was.
- **No accepted data could hold the values.** They are each top's by design, a project's own top among them.
- **What this leaves to the review.** A poor value still gets the hold. Whether a text can be read where the piece is shown is graded all the same, and that grade guards the case.

### What it costs

- **A narrower hold,** for a bar that covers `RS-GRAPHIC-005`. The verdict ends no loop on a piece that names no medium, or whose top declares none for a medium it names.
- **No hold at all under such a bar, until the command can tell.** That takes in a bar over `GRAPHIC` whole, on every piece, from the check's landing to the follow-up's. The check the calibration waited for leaves its rule's hold waiting on one more piece of work.
- **A question to the asker** wherever a fix would change where the piece is shown.
- **A guard no command keeps.** A designer that changes a piece's media against point 4 is found only by the person it tells.
- **The reviewer's sums from the frames stay untested** by a calibration, where a check of boxes did not measure.

### Alternatives considered

- **The clause kept in the record of the check of a text's size, for that check alone.** Rejected: the reason reaches every check that takes a defect from a rule's grades. The work on the command would look for it under the hold ("Why a record of its own").
- **Every check the reviewer's body reads narrows the hold, the checks of boxes among them.** Not taken: their not measuring leaves the grade's content as it was, and happens on few keys. It would switch the hold off for a piece under a brand that gives no step of space, for no gain in evidence.
- **The hold reaching every piece, with the gap named in the review's report.** Not taken. It edits nothing in DEC-042. But a loop would end by the verdict on a piece whose small texts nothing compared. Its evidence was gathered beside a check that compared them.
- **A calibration in each state,** with evidence for each. Rejected: two ladders and two sets of evidence for one rule, to let loops end where a piece does not say where it is shown.
- **The hold off for a whole project while any of its pieces is unmeasured.** Rejected: one old draft would end no loop on any piece, and a piece's state is read at its own round.
- **The command left as built, and its follow-up ordered before any calibration's report is accepted.** Not taken, though it spares the time in which no bar over the rule gets the hold. Only memory would keep that order, and a slip would end loops on pieces nothing measured.
- **The command taught to tell in the change that lands the check.** Not taken, though no time without the hold would then open. Where the command reads the data needs a record of its own (point 6). The check, which the calibration waits for, would wait on that record too.
- **The ladder's designs left as first drawn, a text under its minimum among them.** Rejected: the outside judge would rank down what the review cannot grade.
- **A piece's media kept where no agent writes, such as the capability's configuration.** Rejected: where a piece is shown differs by piece, and the designer writes a piece's file whole from the asker's words.
- **A digest of the media the asker has seen, kept in the brief for the command to compare.** Not taken here. It would let a command find a change the asker never saw, as the makers' record of drafts does for words. It adds a field and a comparison for a guard no trial has shown to fail. The Implications name when to weigh it again.
- **The values a check measures against compared with the accepted data, to lapse the hold.** Rejected ("Why the values such a check measures against lapse no hold").
