**In plain terms:** a brand may hold components of its own, such as a badge no shared library has, and each may declare component tokens, such as `component.platform.badge.fill` in a brand named `platform`. The brand gives such a token a value in its own token files, at that path, whether or not it is built on a base, since no base holds the badge. A token it holds under `component.platform` that names no token of its components, or is of another type, gives nothing and is a finding: it fails the check of a brand version not yet released, and asks for attention in a released one. A base opens its own components' tokens as it opens any role, so a brand built on it, such as a game built on the platform, restyles one of the platform's parts alone, and so does a brand further along the chain, naming no brand but its base. Under a brand outside that chain, the badge draws its tokens at their defaults, in that brand's values wherever a default leads to a role that brand gives a value.

## Context

The record of component tokens lets a brand's own components declare and read their tokens as a library's components do, at `component.<brand>.<component>.<name>` ([visual-communication:DEC-035-component-tokens] points 1 to 4). A brand reaches the component tokens of a library's components only where it names the library. Its tokens under `component.<library>` are judged as its tokens under that library's name: where it names the library, one that names no token its components declare, or is of another type, is a finding ([visual-communication:DEC-008-library-role-defaults] point 4, DEC-035 point 7). A brand names no library for its own components. So DEC-035 point 7 keeps a brand's tokens under `component.<its own name>` private, with no finding, and leaves two questions to the change that builds a brand's own components ([project-kit#2573](https://github.com/aleskalfas/project-kit/issues/2573)): what gives those components' tokens values, and how the check judges a brand's tokens under its own name.

Four facts bear on the answer:
- **A brand's part reads roles, never the brand's private tokens.** A component names roles, and only a brand holds a value ([visual-communication:DEC-001-visual-communication-discipline] point 5). A brand's private tokens, such as a palette, are never exported and never open ([visual-communication:DEC-004-brands-and-notations] point 5).
- **A base opens what a brand built on it may change.** A base lists the roles a brand built on it may give values, component tokens among them, and a value given to any other role fails. Along a chain of brands each link opens only what is open to it, and its own. A brand built on a base keeps the base's parts, and may add parts of its own with their component tokens (DEC-004 point 6).
- **A brand's component may be drawn under another brand.** A top a brand holds is drawn under another brand in that brand's values ([visual-communication:DEC-034-brand-drawings] point 4), and a piece may place a part another brand holds (DEC-034, Rationale for point 4).
- **Whether a brand may name a brand other than its base is open** (DEC-034, Implications). Format 1 accepts such an entry among the libraries a brand builds on, and no record gives it a meaning.

The capability as built resolves a brand with the component tokens of component libraries alone. A brand's own component that reads its own token finds no role at its path, which the engine reports. The token's default is judged nowhere, and a base whose open roles name one opens nothing, and fails.

## Decision

**A brand gives its own components' tokens values under `component.<its own name>`, whether or not it has a base, and the check judges its tokens there as under the name of a library it names. A base opens them as it opens any role, and a brand along its chain gives one a value only where its base opens it to it. Under any other brand, they take their defaults.**

### 1. A brand gives its own components' tokens values, whether or not it has a base

- **Its own to give and to open.** A brand gives values to the component tokens of the components it holds, its drawings and a top it holds among them, opens them and aliases them. It names no library for them, since they are its own. Built on a base, it does so without its base opening them: its base holds none of its components, and opens none of their tokens. Every other role a brand built on a base gives a value stays as DEC-004 points 6 and 11 and DEC-008 point 4 have it, given only where its base opens it.
- **At the token's path.** Each token the brand holds under `component.<its own name>` gives one of its components' tokens a value of its type. One that names no token they declare, or is of another type, gives nothing and is a finding (point 4), as one under the name of a library it names is (DEC-035 point 7).
- **Resolved as any component token is** (DEC-035 point 4). The brand's value wins in the combinations its sources cover. Elsewhere the token's default holds, and takes the brand's value for the role it aliases.
- **A default aliases a role of a library the brand builds on,** the shared roles included, as DEC-035 point 4 lets a default alias, the brand standing as the component's library. It never aliases a component token, nor a token the brand keeps private, which is no role (DEC-004 point 5).
- **Exported as any component token is.** The brand exports those it gives values (DEC-004 point 5), and the document of resolved values holds each, at the brand's value or at its default (DEC-008 point 5).

### 2. A base opens its own components' tokens, and a brand along its chain gives one a value only where it is open to it

A brand's *chain of bases* is its base, and each brand that base builds on as its base in turn (DEC-004 point 6). A brand *along its chain* is one of them.
- **A base opens them as it opens any role.** It opens a token of its own components, or a group of them by whole segments of their paths, such as `component.<its own name>`, whether or not it gives the token a value, as DEC-035 point 2 has a base open a token of a library it names.
- **Each link narrows.** A brand opens a token of a component of a brand along its chain only where its base opens it to it (DEC-004 point 6).
- **A value only where it is open.** A brand gives such a token a value only where its base opens it to it. The base's value comes first, then the brand's own, in the combinations its sources cover, and every token that aliases it follows (DEC-004 point 6).
- **An alias wherever its base exports it.** A brand aliases such a token where its base exports it, open or not, as its aliases reach whatever its base exports (DEC-004 point 6). Opening says what a brand may give a value, never what it may alias.
- **No brand of the chain is named.** A brand reaches the tokens of its chain's components through its base, and names no brand of the chain but its base for them. Naming one of them too adds nothing.
- **Judged as under a library it names.** A token a brand holds under `component.<the name of a brand along its chain>` that names no token of that brand's components, or is of another type, gives nothing, and is a finding. One that names a token its base does not open to it gives nothing too, as a value for any role its base does not open (DEC-004 point 11). Either way the base's value, or the token's default, holds.

### 3. Under any other brand, a brand's components take their tokens' defaults

- **Outside the chain.** Where a piece's brand neither holds a component nor has the brand that holds it along its chain of bases, the component draws each of its tokens at its default. So do a top a brand holds, drawn under another brand (DEC-034 point 4), and a part of a brand that a piece places.
- **In that brand's values where the default leads to one.** Such a token draws in the piece's brand's values only where its default's chain ends at a role that brand gives a value (DEC-035 point 4). Where it ends at none, the part reads a token with no value under that brand, which the check finds at the lock, and which fails there (DEC-035 point 9).
- **The holding brand's values stay its own.** Its value for one of its components' tokens holds only under it and under each brand that has it along its chain of bases (point 2). Where a piece places a part of a brand outside its own brand's chain, and that brand gives one of the part's tokens a value, the check asks the piece's author for attention (point 4), as DEC-034 point 4 does for a top.
- **The piece's brand gives them nothing.** A token it holds under `component.<the holding brand's name>` stays private, as one under a library it does not name, and asks its owner for attention where a piece's lock holds the holding brand with it (DEC-035 point 7).
- **Naming the holding brand reaches nothing.** A brand that names a brand outside its chain among the libraries it builds on reaches none of that brand's components' tokens, and a token it holds under that brand's name stays private, as the bullet before has it. So it stays until the question DEC-034 leaves open, whether a brand may name a brand other than its base, is decided.

### 4. What the checks report, and how severely

Each finding below fails validation where it lies in a file the project can correct ([visual-communication:DEC-002-files-and-validation] point 7). Elsewhere, as in a released brand version, it asks for attention and names who can correct it.
- **Where each lies:**
  - A finding on a brand's own component's tokens and their defaults lies in that component's file in the brand's version, as DEC-035 point 9 places one in its component's version.
  - A part that reads a token with no value under the piece's brand is found at the lock, as DEC-035 point 9 finds it, since only the lock's combination shows it.
  - A finding on a brand's tokens under `component.<its own name>`, or under `component.<the name of a brand along its chain>`, lies in the brand's version, judged when the brand is checked alone, as DEC-008 point 9 places a brand's finding against a library it names.
  - A brand's token under `component.<the name of a brand outside its chain>` is found at the lock that holds that brand with it, since only a lock shows it, as DEC-008 point 9 finds one under a library the brand does not name.
  - A part that a piece places, of a brand outside its brand's chain, is found at the piece, as DEC-034 point 10 places a finding on a piece.
- **Fails validation:**
  - each finding DEC-035 point 9 makes of a component's tokens and their defaults, on a brand's own component's
  - a brand's token under `component.<its own name>`, or under `component.<the name of a brand along its chain>`, that names no token of that brand's components, or is of another type
  - a brand's token that gives a value to a token of a component of a brand along its chain that its base does not open to it (DEC-004 point 11)
- **Asks for attention, never failing:**
  - a brand's token under `component.<the name of a brand outside its chain>`, where a piece's lock holds that brand with it, whether or not the brand names that brand (point 3), naming the brand's owner
  - a part that a piece places, of a brand outside its brand's chain, where the brand that holds the part gives one of its tokens a value, naming the part, the token and both brands, for the piece's author (point 3)
  - a token that a drawing's file reads nowhere, where its brand neither gives it a value nor opens it, naming the brand's owner, as for an experimental component's (DEC-035 point 9), since a drawing carries no status (DEC-034 point 8)
- **Stated as a fact:** a token that a drawing's file reads nowhere, where its brand gives it a value or opens it, as for a stable component's (DEC-035 point 9).

### 5. What this changes in format 1

- **No schema refuses a file it accepted.** Only the check's judgement of a brand's tokens and open roles changes, as it did for a token under a library's own name (DEC-008 point 8).
- **Judged for the first time.** A token a brand holds under `component.<its own name>`, which stayed private before this record, gives a value or is a finding. So does one under the name of a brand along its chain, which stayed private where the brand did not name that brand. An open role that names a token of a brand's own components opens it, where it opened nothing and failed.
- **Attention that never fails.** A piece that places a part of a brand outside its brand's chain may meet point 4's attention for the first time.
- **No file the capability ships meets a new finding.** The neutral brand it ships holds no component of its own, and its tokens under `component` lie only under `component.foundations`, so no released file the capability ships meets a finding this record adds.

## Rationale

**Point 1, the brand gives its own tokens values.** A brand's part reads roles alone, so a value the brand wants for one of its own parts, which no shared role carries, reaches the part only through a token it declares. The record of component tokens already resolves a brand's own component's tokens as a library's, a brand's value winning (DEC-035 point 4). The holding brand is both that brand and the component's library, so it needs no name for itself. A base opens what a brand built on it may change of the base's, so that no fix in the base hides behind a brand's value (DEC-004, Alternatives considered). A brand's own components are none of its base's, so no fix of the base hides behind a value for one of them, and the base knows none of them to open. Asked to open them, a base would leave every brand built on it unable to style a part of its own, such as a game built on its platform (DEC-004, Context). Judged as under a library it names, a misspelt token fails where the brand's owner looks, rather than giving nothing without a word, the reason DEC-035 gives for its point 7. A brand holds no role of its own in format 1, so its components' defaults alias roles of the libraries it builds on.

**Point 2, the chain.** A base says what a brand built on it may change, and each link narrows what the one before it opens (DEC-004 point 6), so a brand gives a value to a token of a brand further down only where every link between opens it. An alias reads a value and changes none, so it follows what the base exports, as every alias of a brand built on a base does (DEC-004 point 6).

A brand names a library by major so that its values never meet a major they were not tuned for (DEC-008, Rationale). Along a chain, a brand meets a new major of a brand further down only by a move it makes on purpose:
- A brand that moves to a new major of its base makes a breaking change of its own ([visual-communication:DEC-003-change-over-time] point 6), so it moves only in a new major of its own.
- A brand built on it takes that new major only on purpose, as a change of its own, since it names its base by major (DEC-004 point 6).
- A lock refuses a base at a major other than the one its brand names, and holds one version of each library (DEC-004 point 6, DEC-003 point 8).

So the major a brand names of its base fixes the major of every brand along its chain.

The same holds of a library a base names, yet the base's naming it does not spare a brand built on it from naming it too (DEC-008 point 4). What sets a brand of the chain apart is how closely it ties a brand to it. A brand's chain is its line of descent: a brand keeps its base's parts, its layouts and every value it is not open to change, and its base keeps its own base's in turn (DEC-004 point 6). So a part a brand along its chain holds is part of the form the brand draws, as its own parts are, and being built on a brand ties a brand to it more closely than naming a library does. A library its base names is a side dependency of the base, named only to give its roles values (DEC-008 point 4), and none of its parts is part of the brand's form. So a library named along the chain stays one that each brand names to give its roles values, and a brand of the chain needs no naming. Asking for it would also ask a brand to name a brand other than its base, which DEC-034 leaves open.

**Point 3, the defaults elsewhere.** A piece is drawn in one brand's values. The holding brand's value for its token, drawn under another brand, would mix two brands' looks in one piece, which the record of a library's own roles refuses (DEC-008, Alternatives considered). The default draws the component in the piece's brand's values, as DEC-034 point 4 draws a top a brand holds, wherever the default leads to a role that brand gives a value. Where it leads to none, the part draws nothing under that brand, a fault only the combination shows, so the lock finds it (DEC-035 point 9). The cost is a part that loses the look its holder gave it. The piece's author learns of it as DEC-034 point 4 has them learn it of a top, and only where the holder gave one of the part's tokens a value, since only there is a look of the holder's lost. Naming a brand outside the chain reaches nothing, since what such naming means is the question DEC-034 leaves open, and a reach given here would decide it in passing.

**Point 4, the severities.** The project corrects its own brand's version, so a finding there fails (DEC-002 point 7). A drawing's token that its file reads nowhere breaks nothing, and nothing promises a drawing's tokens (DEC-034 point 8). Where its brand neither gives it a value nor opens it, its owner may drop it at once, as an experimental component's owner may, so the owner is asked to (DEC-035, Rationale for point 9). Once its brand gives it a value it is exported, and once its brand opens it it is open, and removing either breaks (DEC-004 point 5). So it is then a lawful state until the brand's next major, stated as a fact, as a stable component's unread token is. A brand's token under the name of a brand outside its chain is shown only by a lock that holds both, so it lies at that lock, as one under a library the brand does not name does (DEC-008 point 9). A part of a brand outside the chain is the piece's author's choice, so the attention names them, and it never fails, since format 1 accepted the piece (DEC-003 point 3).

### Alternatives considered

- **The holding brand's own tokens always at their defaults under it,** given values only by the brands built on it: a brand could give its own part no value that no shared role carries, and a base could not give its own part the look it opens to the brands built on it.
- **A brand's own components' tokens given values only where its base opens them,** as the open roles have it for every other role: no base holds them, so none could open them, and no brand built on a base could give a part of its own a look of its own.
- **A brand's own part reading the brand's private tokens:** a component would read a value, not a role (DEC-001 point 5). The private tokens a base reshapes freely would become a promise, and a brand built on it, which reaches only what its base opens, could never restyle that part.
- **A brand's tokens under `component.<its own name>` kept private:** a misspelt token would give nothing without a word.
- **One that names no token asking only for attention:** the project corrects its own brand, and DEC-008 point 9 fails the same mistake under a library the brand names.
- **A brand naming each brand of its chain by major,** as it names a library: it keeps DEC-008 point 4, and [visual-communication:DEC-009-layout-defaults] point 3 for a top's layouts, as they read, and format 1 already accepts the entry. It would decide a narrow part of the question DEC-034 leaves open, naming a brand of one's own chain. Its cost: each brand keeps two majors or more in step, its base's and each further brand's, which the major of its base already fixes (Rationale for point 2), and its lock fails where they drift.
- **Reach without naming for a library named along the chain too:** a brand would give values to the roles of a library its base names, where its base opens them, without naming it. It agrees with point 2, but it widens DEC-008 point 4 for every library, beyond the brand's own components this record answers for, and it takes a side dependency of the base for part of the brand's line of descent, which the Rationale for point 2 tells apart.
- **A brand reaching only its base's own components' tokens:** a brand keeps the parts of every brand along its chain (DEC-004 point 6), yet it could never restyle one held two links away or more, even where each link between opens it, such as a game's tournament edition restyling a control of the platform the game is built on (DEC-004, Rationale). A library its base names stays within its reach, since it may name the library too (DEC-008 point 4). A brand two links away or more it could not name, since naming a brand other than its base is left open (DEC-034).
- **The holding brand's values following its components under any brand:** for the reason the Rationale gives under point 3.
- **A part of a brand outside the chain drawn at its defaults with nothing said:** the piece's author would not learn that a look the part's holder gave it is lost, as DEC-034 point 4 has them learn it of a top.
- **A drawing's unread token always asking for attention:** it would ask the owner to drop a token whose removal breaks, once its brand gives it a value or opens it (DEC-004 point 5).
