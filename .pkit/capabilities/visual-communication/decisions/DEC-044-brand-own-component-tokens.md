**In plain terms:** a brand may hold components of its own, such as a badge no shared library has, and each may declare component tokens, such as `component.platform.badge.fill` in a brand named `platform`. The brand gives such a token a value in its own token files, at that path, whether or not it is built on a base, since no base holds the badge. A token it holds under `component.platform` that names no token of its components, or is of another type, gives nothing and is a finding: it fails the check of a brand version not yet released, and asks for attention in a released one. A base opens its own components' tokens as it opens any role. So a game built on the platform may give the badge's fill a value of its own where the platform opens it, and so may the game's tournament edition, built on the game, where the game opens it in turn, without naming any brand but its own base. Under a brand that is neither the platform nor built on it, the badge draws its tokens at their defaults, in that brand's values wherever a default leads to a role that brand gives a value.

## Context

The record of component tokens lets a brand's own components declare and read their tokens as a library's components do, at `component.<brand>.<component>.<name>` ([visual-communication:DEC-035-component-tokens] points 1 to 4). A brand reaches the component tokens of a library's components only where it names the library. Its tokens under `component.<library>` are judged as its tokens under that library's name: where it names the library, one that names no token its components declare, or is of another type, is a finding ([visual-communication:DEC-008-library-role-defaults] point 4, DEC-035 point 7). A brand names no library for its own components. So DEC-035 point 7 keeps a brand's tokens under `component.<its own name>` private, with no finding, and leaves two questions to the change that builds a brand's own components ([project-kit#2573](https://github.com/aleskalfas/project-kit/issues/2573)): what gives those components' tokens values, and how the check judges a brand's tokens under its own name.

Four facts bear on the answer:
- **A brand's part reads roles, never the brand's private tokens.** A component names roles, and only a brand holds a value ([visual-communication:DEC-001-visual-communication-discipline] point 5). A brand's private tokens, such as a palette, are never exported and never open ([visual-communication:DEC-004-brands-and-notations] point 5).
- **A base opens what a brand built on it may change.** A base lists the roles a brand built on it may give values, component tokens among them, and a value given to any other role fails. Along a chain of brands each link opens only what is open to it, and its own. A brand built on a base keeps the base's parts, and may add roles of its own, and parts of its own with their component tokens (DEC-004 point 6).
- **A brand's component may be drawn under another brand.** A top a brand holds is drawn under another brand in that brand's values ([visual-communication:DEC-034-brand-drawings] point 4), and a piece may place a part another brand holds (DEC-034, Rationale for point 4).
- **Whether a brand may name a brand other than its base is open** (DEC-034, Implications). Format 1 accepts such an entry among the libraries a brand builds on, and no record gives it a meaning.

The capability as built resolves a brand with the component tokens of component libraries alone, and in format 1 it reads no brand's own roles. A brand's own component that reads its own token finds no role at its path, which the engine reports. The token's default is judged nowhere, and a base whose open roles name one opens nothing, and fails.

## Decision

**A brand gives values to its own roles and its own components' tokens, whether or not it has a base, and the check judges its tokens under `component.<its own name>` as under `component.<library>` for any library whose roles it reaches. A base opens them as it opens any role, and a brand that has that base along its chain gives one a value only where its own base opens it to it, without naming any brand of the chain but its base. Under any other brand, they take their defaults.**

### 1. A brand gives values to its own roles and its components' tokens, whether or not it has a base

The roles a brand *adds* are its own roles (DEC-008 point 1) and the component tokens of the components it holds, its drawings and a top it holds among them (DEC-004 point 6). No base holds them.
- **Its own to give and to open.** A brand gives values to the roles it adds, opens them and aliases them. It names no library for them, since they are its own. Built on a base, it does so without its base opening them: its base holds none of them, and opens none. Every other role a brand built on a base gives a value stays as DEC-004 points 6 and 11 and DEC-008 point 4 have it, given a value only where its base opens it.
- **At the token's path.** Each token the brand holds under `component.<its own name>` gives one of its components' tokens a value of its type. One that names no token they declare, or is of another type, gives nothing and is a finding (point 4), as one under `component.<library>` is for any other library whose roles it reaches (DEC-008 point 4, DEC-035 point 7).
- **Under its own name, outside `component`, a token asks nothing.** Until the capability reads a brand's own roles, every token the brand holds there is private and asks nothing, and a default or an open role that names one names no role, and fails (point 4). One under the name of any other library whose roles it reaches is a finding (DEC-008 point 4). A brand may keep private tokens there, beside its own roles (DEC-035 point 7), so how the check tells a misspelt role from a private token is left to the change that comes to read a brand's own roles.
- **Resolved as any component token is** (DEC-035 point 4). The brand's value wins in the combinations its sources cover. Elsewhere the token's default holds, and takes the brand's value for the role it aliases.
- **A default aliases a role of a library whose roles the brand reaches** (DEC-008 point 4), its own roles and the shared roles included, as DEC-035 point 4 lets a default alias, the brand standing as the component's library. It never aliases a component token, nor a token the brand keeps private, which is no role (DEC-004 point 5), nor a role of a brand outside its chain that the brand names (point 3).
- **Exported as any component token is.** The brand exports those it gives values (DEC-004 point 5), and the document of resolved values holds each, at the brand's value or at its default (DEC-008 point 5).

### 2. A base opens the roles it adds, and a brand that has it along its chain gives one a value only where its own base opens it

A brand's *chain of bases* is its base, and each brand that base builds on as its base in turn (DEC-004 point 6). A brand *along its chain* is one of them, so the phrase runs from a brand toward the root of its chain, the brand there that has no base. A brand *outside its chain* is any other brand: neither the brand itself nor one along its chain.
- **A base opens them as it opens any role.** It opens a role it adds, or a group of them by whole segments of their paths, such as `component.<its own name>`, whether or not it gives the role a value, as DEC-035 point 2 has a base open a token of a library whose roles it reaches.
- **Each link narrows.** A brand opens a role of a brand along its chain only where its base opens it to it (DEC-004 point 6).
- **A value only where it is open.** A brand gives such a role a value only where its base opens it to it. The base's value comes first, then the brand's own, in the combinations its sources cover, and every token that aliases it follows (DEC-004 point 6).
- **An alias wherever its base exports it.** A brand aliases such a role where its base exports it, open or not, as its aliases reach whatever its base exports (DEC-004 point 6). Opening says what a brand may give a value, never what it may alias.
- **Reached through its base.** A brand reaches the roles of each brand along its chain, their own roles and their components' tokens alike, through its base, as the bullets before have it, without naming any of those brands but its base. It uses their names through its base too, and so places the parts of theirs it keeps (DEC-004 point 6), naming none of them but its base. It may name one of them beyond its base too, as format 1 accepts. That adds only that the major it names agree with the one its base fixes, since a lock holds one version of each library, those a brand builds on included (DEC-003 point 8), and so holds that brand whether or not the brand names it. The brand reaches, places, owes and is judged as it is without that name.
- **Judged as under any library whose roles it reaches.** A token a brand holds under the name of a brand along its chain, or under `component.<that brand's name>`, that names none of that brand's roles, or is of another type, gives nothing, and is a finding, whether or not the brand names that brand. One that names a role its base does not open to it gives nothing too, as a value for any role its base does not open (DEC-004 point 11). Either way the base's value, or the role's default, holds.

### 3. Under any other brand, a brand's components take their tokens' defaults

- **Outside the chain.** Where a piece's brand neither holds a component nor has the brand that holds it along its chain, the component draws each of its tokens at its default, and each of the holding brand's own roles it reads at that role's default (DEC-008 point 4). So do a top a brand holds, drawn under another brand (DEC-034 point 4), and a part of a brand that a piece places.
- **In that brand's values where the default leads to one.** Such a token draws in the piece's brand's values only where its default's chain ends at a role that brand gives a value (DEC-035 point 4). Where it ends at none, the part reads a token with no value under that brand, which the check finds at the lock, and which fails there (DEC-035 point 9).
- **The holding brand's values stay its own.** Its value for one of its components' tokens holds only under it and under each brand that has it along its chain (point 2). Where a piece places a part of a brand outside its own brand's chain, and that brand gives one of the part's tokens a value, the check asks the piece's author for attention (point 4), as DEC-034 point 4 does for a top.
- **The piece's brand gives them nothing.** A token it holds under the holding brand's name, or under `component.<that name>`, stays private, as one under a library whose roles it does not reach, and asks its owner for attention where a piece's lock holds the holding brand with it (DEC-008 point 9, DEC-035 point 7).
- **Naming the holding brand reaches nothing.** A brand that names a brand outside its chain among the libraries it builds on reaches none of that brand's roles, and owes it nothing. A token it holds under that brand's name stays private, as the bullet before has it, and an open role that names one of that brand's roles fails (point 4). So it stays until the question DEC-034 leaves open, whether a brand may name a brand other than its base, is decided.

### 4. What the checks report, and how severely

Each finding below fails validation where it lies in a file the project can correct ([visual-communication:DEC-002-files-and-validation] point 7). Elsewhere, as in a released brand version, it asks for attention and names who can correct it.
- **Where each lies:**
  - A finding on a brand's own component's tokens and their defaults lies in that component's file in the brand's version, as DEC-035 point 9 places one in its component's version.
  - A part that reads a token with no value under the piece's brand, where that brand does not reach the roles of the token's library, is found at the lock, as DEC-035 point 9 finds it, since only the lock's combination shows it.
  - A finding on a brand's tokens under `component.<its own name>`, or under the name of a brand along its chain or `component.<that name>`, or on its open roles, lies in the brand's version, judged when the brand is checked alone, as DEC-008 point 9 places a brand's finding against a library whose roles it reaches.
  - A brand's token under the name of a brand outside its chain, or under `component.<that name>`, is found at the lock that holds that brand with it, since only a lock shows it, as DEC-008 point 9 finds one under a library whose roles the brand does not reach.
  - A part that a piece places, of a brand outside its brand's chain, is found at the piece, as DEC-034 point 10 places a finding on a piece.
- **Fails validation:**
  - each finding DEC-035 point 9 makes of a component's tokens and their defaults, on a brand's own component's, a library whose roles the brand does not reach standing for one its library does not build on, and a default that names a token the brand keeps private for one that leads nowhere (point 1)
  - a brand's token under `component.<its own name>`, or under `component.<the name of a brand along its chain>`, that names no token of that brand's components, or is of another type
  - a brand's token under the name of a brand along its chain, outside `component`, that names none of that brand's roles, or is of another type, whether or not the brand names that brand
  - a brand's token that gives a value to a role of a brand along its chain that its base does not open to it (DEC-004 point 11)
  - an open role, under the brand's own name or under `component.<its own name>`, that names none of the roles the brand adds (DEC-004 point 11)
  - an open role that opens a role of a brand along its chain that its base does not open to it (DEC-004 point 11)
  - an open role that names a role of a brand outside its chain, whose roles the brand does not reach (point 3)
- **Asks for attention, never failing:**
  - a brand's token under the name of a brand outside its chain, or under `component.<that name>`, where a piece's lock holds that brand with it, whether or not the brand names that brand (point 3), naming the brand's owner
  - a part that a piece places, of a brand outside its brand's chain, where the brand that holds the part gives one of its tokens a value, naming the part, the token and both brands, for the piece's author (point 3)
  - a token that a drawing's file reads nowhere, where its brand neither gives it a value nor opens it, naming the brand's owner, as for an experimental component's (DEC-035 point 9), since a drawing carries no status (DEC-034 point 8)
- **Stated as a fact:** a token that a drawing's file reads nowhere, where its brand gives it a value or opens it, as for a stable component's (DEC-035 point 9).
- **No finding:** a brand's token under its own name, outside `component`, until the capability reads a brand's own roles (point 1).

### 5. What this changes in format 1

- **No schema refuses a file it accepted.** Only the check's judgement of a brand's tokens and open roles changes, as it did for a token under a library's own name (DEC-008 point 8).
- **Judged for the first time.** A token a brand holds under `component.<its own name>`, which stayed private before this record, gives a value or is a finding. So does one under `component.<the name of a brand along its chain>`, which stayed private where the brand did not name that brand. One under the name of a brand along its chain, outside `component`, which asked only for attention where the brand did not name that brand, is a finding too. An open role that names a token of a brand's own components opens it, where it opened nothing and failed. An open role that names a role of a brand along its chain opens it where its base opens it to it, where it failed before if the brand did not name that brand. One that names a role of a brand outside its chain fails, even where the brand names that brand.
- **What a group opens widens.** A base that opens the group `component`, released or not, comes to open its own components' tokens, and the tokens of its chain open to it, without a change to its file. Taking one of them off its open roles then breaks, as for any open role (DEC-004 point 5).
- **What a group opens narrows, for a brand outside the chain.** A released base that names a brand outside its chain comes to open none of that brand's tokens, without a change to its file, and it and a brand built on it lose the values they gave them, a narrowing DEC-004 point 5 counts as breaking. It is dormant: no brand the capability ships names another, and the capability as built resolves no brand's component tokens and reads no brand's own roles (Context).
- **Attention that never fails.** A piece that places a part of a brand outside its brand's chain may meet point 4's attention for the first time.

## Rationale

**Point 1, the brand gives the roles it adds values.** A brand's part reads roles alone, so a value the brand wants for one of its own parts, which no shared role carries, comes to the part only through a token it declares, or a role of its own. The record of component tokens already resolves a brand's own component's tokens as a library's, a brand's value winning (DEC-035 point 4). The holding brand is both that brand and the component's library, so it needs no name for itself. A base opens what a brand built on it may change of the base's, so that no fix in the base hides behind a brand's value (DEC-004, Alternatives considered). A role a brand adds is an addition, and the record of brands and notations already lets a brand add roles and parts for that reason: "An addition such as a role or a part cannot hide a fix in the base. A replacement would." (DEC-004, Rationale for why a brand built on another adds no axis). So a value for a role a brand adds needs no leave of the base's, and the base, which holds none of them, knows none to open. Asked to open them, a base would leave every brand built on it unable to style a part of its own, such as a game built on its platform (DEC-004, Context). Judged as under `component.<library>` for any library whose roles it reaches, a misspelt token fails where the brand's owner looks, rather than giving nothing without a word, the reason DEC-035 gives for its point 7. Under its own name, outside `component`, a brand keeps its own roles, and may keep private tokens beside them (DEC-035 point 7). Judged as under a library's name, each private token there would fail. The capability reads no brand's own roles yet (Context), so it knows each token there only as a private one, and asks nothing of it, until the change that reads a brand's own roles decides how to tell a misspelt role from a private token.

**Point 2, the chain.** A base says what a brand built on it may change, and each link narrows what the one before it opens (DEC-004 point 6), so a brand gives a value to a role of a brand along its chain only where every link between opens it. An alias reads a value and changes none, so it follows what the base exports, as every alias of a brand built on a base does (DEC-004 point 6).

A brand names a library by major so that its values never meet a major they were not tuned for (DEC-008, Rationale). Along a chain, a brand meets a new major of a brand along it only by a move it makes on purpose:
- A brand that moves to a new major of its base makes a breaking change of its own ([visual-communication:DEC-003-change-over-time] point 6), so it moves only in a new major of its own.
- A brand built on it takes that new major only on purpose, as a change of its own, since it names its base by major (DEC-004 point 6).
- A lock refuses a base at a major other than the one its brand names, and holds one version of each library (DEC-004 point 6, DEC-003 point 8).

So the major a brand names of its base fixes the major of every brand along its chain. The release's comparison already counts a library it builds on, taken at a new major, as a break (DEC-003 point 6), so each link's move is found when its release is compared.

The same holds of a library a base names, yet the base's naming it does not spare a brand built on it from naming it too (DEC-008 point 4). What sets a brand of the chain apart is whom its parts are for:
- **A brand's parts** are the few that no shared component has (DEC-001 point 5). They are there for the brand and the brands built on it, which keep them (DEC-004 point 6), and they lose their look under any other brand (point 3). Its own roles serve only its parts and the brands built on it too, since no component library's component reads them without naming the brand.
- **A component library's parts** are meant for every brand (DEC-034, Rationale for point 4).

Naming is how a brand opts into a shared library, and a chain is how a brand inherits a brand. So a library named along the chain stays one that each brand opts into, and names to give its roles values. A brand along the chain is inherited with the base, and needs no naming. Asking for it would also ask a brand to name a brand other than its base, which DEC-034 leaves open.

A token under the name of a brand along its chain is judged as one under its base's name, whether or not the brand names that brand. So a misspelt one fails where the brand's owner looks, and naming a brand of its chain beyond its base adds only that the major it names agree with the one its base fixes.

**Point 3, the defaults elsewhere.** A piece is drawn in one brand's values. The holding brand's value for its token, drawn under another brand, would mix two brands' looks in one piece, which the record of a library's own roles refuses (DEC-008, Alternatives considered). The default draws the component in the piece's brand's values, as DEC-034 point 4 draws a top a brand holds, wherever the default leads to a role that brand gives a value. Where it leads to none, the part draws nothing under that brand, a fault only the combination shows, so the lock finds it (DEC-035 point 9). The cost is a part that loses the look its holder gave it. The piece's author learns of it as DEC-034 point 4 has them learn it of a top, and only where the holder gave one of the part's tokens a value, since only there is a look of the holder's lost. Naming a brand outside the chain reaches nothing, since what such naming means is the question DEC-034 leaves open, and letting such naming reach a brand's roles here would decide it in passing.

**Point 4, the severities.** The project corrects its own brand's version, so a finding there fails (DEC-002 point 7). A drawing's token that its file reads nowhere breaks nothing, and nothing promises a drawing's tokens (DEC-034 point 8). Where its brand neither gives it a value nor opens it, its owner may drop it at once, as an experimental component's owner may, so the owner is asked to (DEC-035, Rationale for point 9). Once its brand gives it a value it is exported, and once its brand opens it it is open, and removing either breaks (DEC-004 point 5). So it is then a lawful state until the brand's next major, stated as a fact, as a stable component's unread token is. A brand's token under the name of a brand outside its chain is shown only by a lock that holds both, so it lies at that lock, as one under a library whose roles the brand does not reach does (DEC-008 point 9). A part of a brand outside the chain is the piece's author's choice, so the attention names them, and it never fails, since format 1 accepted the piece (DEC-003 point 3).

### Alternatives considered

- **The holding brand's own tokens always at their defaults under it,** given values only by the brands built on it: a brand could give its own part no value that no shared role carries, and a base could not give its own part the look it opens to the brands built on it.
- **The roles a brand adds given values only where its base opens them,** as the open roles have it for every other role: no base holds them, so none could open them, and no brand built on a base could give a part of its own a look of its own.
- **A brand's own part reading the brand's private tokens:** a component would read a value, not a role (DEC-001 point 5). The private tokens a base reshapes freely would become a promise, and a brand built on it, which gives values only to what its base opens, could never restyle that part.
- **A brand's tokens under `component.<its own name>` kept private:** a misspelt token would give nothing without a word.
- **One that names no token asking only for attention:** the project corrects its own brand, and DEC-008 point 9 fails the same mistake under a library the brand names.
- **A token under the name of a brand along its chain judged only where the brand names that brand:** a misspelt one under a brand beyond its base would give nothing without a word, and naming that brand would add more than that the major it names agree with the one its base fixes, since it would change what the check judges.
- **A brand naming each brand of its chain by major,** as it names a library: it keeps DEC-008 point 4, and [visual-communication:DEC-009-layout-defaults] point 3 for a top's layouts, as they read, and format 1 already accepts the entry. It would decide more of the question DEC-034 leaves open: that a brand must name each brand of its own chain, and that the naming reaches its roles. Its cost: each brand keeps two majors or more in step, its base's and that of each brand along its chain beyond its base, which the major of its base already fixes (Rationale for point 2), and its lock fails where they drift.
- **Reach without naming for a library named along the chain too:** a brand would give values to the roles of a library its base names, where its base opens them, without naming it. It agrees with point 2, but it widens DEC-008 point 4 for every library, beyond the brand's own components this record answers for, and it treats a shared library a base opted into as inherited, which the Rationale for point 2 tells apart.
- **A brand reaching only the roles its base adds:** a brand keeps the parts of every brand along its chain (DEC-004 point 6), yet it could never restyle one held two links away or more, even where each link between opens it, such as a game's tournament edition restyling a control of the platform the game is built on (DEC-004, Rationale). It may still reach the roles of a library its base names, by naming the library too (DEC-008 point 4). A brand two links away or more it could not name, since naming a brand other than its base is left open (DEC-034).
- **The holding brand's values following its components under any brand:** for the reason the Rationale gives under point 3.
- **A part of a brand outside the chain drawn at its defaults with nothing said:** the piece's author would not learn that a look the part's holder gave it is lost, as DEC-034 point 4 has them learn it of a top.
- **A drawing's unread token always asking for attention:** it would ask the owner to drop a token whose removal breaks, once its brand gives it a value or opens it (DEC-004 point 5).

## Implications

- **What this decides of the question DEC-034 leaves open,** whether a brand may name a brand other than its base. It decides outright that a brand may name a brand along its chain beyond its base, that naming it adds only that the major it names agree with the one its base fixes, and that the brand places the parts it keeps of that brand whether or not it names it (point 2). It holds, until that question is decided, that naming a brand outside its chain reaches nothing (point 3), and says nothing of what such naming means once it is, nor of placing that brand's parts.
- **Edits to accepted records,** made in this change once this record is accepted, each citing it with the pointer "(refinement per [visual-communication:DEC-044-brand-own-component-tokens])", with the point that decides it. Accepting this record accepts them (COR-066 point 5). Inside each quote, a point is the edited record's own.
  - DEC-001 point 5, its opening paragraph: "It names the libraries it builds on, those whose names it uses or whose values it takes, each by the major version it fits" comes to end "but a brand need not name a brand of its chain of bases beyond its base (DEC-004 point 6), whose names it reaches through its base" (DEC-044 point 2).
  - DEC-004's Decision, its top line: "A brand may build on one other brand, and gives values only to the roles that base declares open" comes to end "and to the roles it adds" (DEC-044 point 1).
  - DEC-004 point 6's heading comes to read "A brand may build on one other brand, within the roles its base declares open and those it adds" (DEC-044 point 1).
  - DEC-004 point 6, "The base declares its open roles": "A value given to any other role fails validation" comes to end "but for a role the brand adds, one of its own roles or a token of its own components, which no base holds, so that the brand gives it a value without its base opening it" (DEC-044 point 1).
  - DEC-004 point 11, "Fails validation": its first item comes to read "a value given to a role its base does not open, other than a role the brand adds (point 6); an open role that names no role, or, for a brand built on a base, a role its base does not open to it, other than one the brand adds; or an alias to a token its base keeps private". So the open-role clause also follows DEC-004 point 6's "each link narrows" for every role, not only a role of a brand along the chain, and fails an open role its base has but does not open to it (DEC-044 points 1, 2 and 4).
  - DEC-008's Decision, its top line: "A brand reaches a library's roles only where it names that library" comes to read "A brand reaches a library's roles only where it names that library, is that library, or has it along its chain of bases" (DEC-044 points 1 to 3).
  - DEC-008 point 4's heading comes to read "A brand reaches a library's roles only where it names the library, is the library, or has it along its chain" (DEC-044 points 1 to 3).
  - DEC-008 point 4, "Named by major," comes to be split in two bullets, the definition first. The first reads: "**What a brand reaches.** To reach a role is to give it a value, open it or alias it. A brand *reaches the roles* of these libraries, the component tokens of their components among them (DEC-004 point 7), and of no other: each library it names among those it builds on, but a brand other than its base; itself, naming no library for its own roles; and each brand along its chain of bases, through its base, without naming any of them but its base. What it may do with each of those roles the rest of this point says." The second keeps the heading "Named by major" and comes to say that a brand names each library it builds on by the major it fits, so that its values never meet roles of a major they were not tuned for; that a brand along its chain needs no naming, since the major the brand names of its base fixes that brand's major; that naming one of them beyond its base adds only that the major it names agree with the one its base fixes; and that naming a brand outside its chain, neither the brand itself nor one along its chain, reaches none of that brand's roles (DEC-044 points 1 to 3).
  - DEC-008 point 4, "What it owes a library it names," comes to be headed "What it owes a library whose roles it reaches by naming it". It keeps its first sentence, and comes to add that naming a brand other than its base asks nothing of the brand. Its sentences on the tokens the brand holds move to the bullet below (DEC-044 points 2 and 3).
  - DEC-008 point 4 gains a bullet after it, reading: "**Its tokens under a library's name.** Each token it holds under the name of a library whose roles it reaches, but its own name, gives one of the library's roles a value of its type, and each under `component.<library>`, for any library whose roles it reaches, its own included, gives one of the library's components' tokens a value of its type. Its tokens under the name of a brand along its chain are judged so whether it names that brand or not. So its private tokens lie elsewhere, or under its own name, outside `component`. Until the capability reads a brand's own roles, every token there is private and asks nothing, and a default or an open role that names one names no role. How the check then tells a misspelt role from a private token is left to the change that comes to read a brand's own roles." (DEC-044 points 1, 2 and 4).
