# Yardstick: the showcase deck's design rounds 1–7

This tree is a yardstick, kept outside the default branch's history. It holds the seven rounds in which a designer and a blocking critic redrew project-kit's showcase deck on 8 November 2026, outside the visual-communication capability, and the deck as the capability drew it on 13 November 2026.

It is never an input to the capability's trials. A session that runs a trial never reads it. A session that judges a trial, or calibrates the capability's review, reads it.

- `rounds/round-N/`: each round's pages, in two languages (`cs`, `en`) and two modes (`light`, `dark`), as SVG and PNG, with the critic's verdict (`verdict.md`) and the designer's notes (`design-rationale.md`, `design-system.md`, `gaps.md`). Round 7 adds its ledger of settled decisions (`ledger.md`) and its changes (`changes.md`). Round 5 is the first in which the critic passed every page; rounds 6 and 7 polished after the pass.
- `briefs/`: the critic's and the designer's briefs (`senior-critic.md`, `senior-designer.md`), the page briefs and the findings on the round plan.
- `main-331d7f58a/`: the capability's drawing of the deck at commit 331d7f58a, through its SVG renderer, in both languages and both modes, with the look's report.
- `swap/`: pages 1 and 2 as main drew them with round 7's values put in (the swap test).

Round 7 is drawn at 1920×1080 from a 1280×720 slide; main's drawing at 1568×882 from the same slide.
